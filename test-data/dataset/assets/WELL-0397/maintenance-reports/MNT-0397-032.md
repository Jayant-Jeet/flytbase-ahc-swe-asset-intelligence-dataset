## What happened

Field staff reported that the pump jack was running but the wellhead was cold and the rod load had dropped. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0397-0218 showed the wellhead at 16.3 °C with the air at 11.0 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. A dynamometer card showed no load from the downhole pump, which points to a parted rod.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 9 days longer than the repair itself because the workover rig is busy on other wells.

## Root cause

A coupling unscrewed after repeated rod buckling at the bottom of the stroke.

## Safety and permits

- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

Summary of the fix: fished and replaced parted rod section.
