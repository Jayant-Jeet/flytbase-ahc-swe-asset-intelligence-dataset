# Maintenance Report — MNT-0096-012

**Asset:** WELL-0096 (SW-24 Well 096, T200, Southwest Block 24)
**Equipment:** flowline
**Work started:** 23 Feb 2024 08:00 · **Finished:** 28 Feb 2024 08:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 120 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Kevin Price, Daniel Foster
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0096-0070 on 2024-02-22
**Report written:** 01 Mar 2024

## What happened

Field staff reported that the inspector saw wet soil above the buried flowline. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0096-0070 showed the wellhead at 16.9 °C with the air at 14.5 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Excavation found a corrosion pinhole on the bottom of the line.
The well stayed shut in for about 3 days longer than the repair itself because the crew was committed to a higher-priority spill response elsewhere in the field.

## Root cause

Internal corrosion from produced water collecting at a low point.
This is corrective repair number 3 on this well's flowline in the last 12 months.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).
- Hot work permit issued for welding; gas test before welding read 0% LEL, below the 10% limit (WS-04).

## Work done

1. Excavated around the leak.
2. Cut out the corroded section.
3. Welded in a new section of line pipe.
4. Coated the new joint.
5. Backfilled after the pressure test.

Summary of the fix: cut out and replaced the corroded flowline section.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure-tested the repaired section at 261 psi (1.25 × the flowline's maximum operating pressure of 209 psi) and held it for 4 hours with no pressure drop (WS-05). The well was returned to production.

## Waste

- Oily soil from the excavation and the cut-out pipe sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0096-012 (WS-06).
