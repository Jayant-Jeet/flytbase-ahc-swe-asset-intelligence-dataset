# Maintenance Report — MNT-0496-027

**Asset:** WELL-0496 (SE-32 Well 496, T500, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 25 Jul 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 12 Aug 2026 03:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 427 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Megan Ward · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0496-0195 on 2026-07-23
**Report written:** 13 Aug 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0496-0195 showed the wellhead at 45.6 °C with the air at 37.8 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 16 days longer than the repair itself because the crew was committed to a higher-priority spill response elsewhere in the field.

## Root cause

Controller float and switch failure.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 2 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level; one crew member's monitor had not been bump-tested that day (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
