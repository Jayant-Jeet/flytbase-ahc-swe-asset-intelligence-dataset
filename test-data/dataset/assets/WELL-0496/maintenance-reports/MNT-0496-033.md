## What happened

Field staff reported that a damp, dark patch appeared along the flowline route. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0496-0216 showed the wellhead at 20.6 °C with the air at 7.5 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Excavation found a corrosion pinhole on the bottom of the line.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 37 days longer than the repair itself because the job has to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Internal corrosion from produced water collecting at a low point.
