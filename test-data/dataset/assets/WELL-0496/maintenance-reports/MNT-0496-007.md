# Maintenance Report — MNT-0496-007

**Asset:** WELL-0496 (SE-32 Well 496, T500, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 01 Dec 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 12 Dec 2023 14:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 270 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Kevin Price, Megan Ward
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0496-0058 on 2023-11-30
**Report written:** 14 Dec 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0496-0058 showed the wellhead at 31.7 °C with the air at 15.7 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 9 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Paraffin build-up stopped the float from moving.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
