# Maintenance Report — MNT-0159-023

**Asset:** WELL-0159 (NE-08 Well 159, T350, Northeast Block 8)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 19 Dec 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 31 Dec 2025 00:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 280 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0159-0164 on 2025-12-18
**Report written:** 01 Jan 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the operator found an oil puddle under the gearbox. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0159-0164 showed the wellhead at 20.4 °C with the air at 10.0 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The input shaft seal was worn and the gearbox oil level was below the sight glass.
The well stayed shut in for about 10 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part was back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Seal wear, made worse by heat and a low oil level.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 4 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Drained the gearbox oil into a closed container.
2. Replaced the input and output shaft seals.
3. Cleaned the base and absorbed the spilled oil.
4. Refilled with the specified gear oil to the correct level.

Summary of the fix: replaced gearbox seals and topped up oil.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 1 hour and checked both shafts for leaks: none found. The well was returned to production.

## Waste

- Used gear oil and oily pads sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0159-023 (WS-06).
