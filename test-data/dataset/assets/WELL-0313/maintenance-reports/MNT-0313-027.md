# Maintenance Report — MNT-0313-027

**Asset:** WELL-0313 (NW-12 Well 313, T350, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 18 Dec 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 06 Jan 2027 12:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 460 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0313-0215 on 2026-12-17
**Report written:** 08 Jan 2027

## What happened

Field staff reported that the electric motor driving the pump jack had stopped and would not restart. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0313-0215 showed the wellhead at 14.1 °C with the air at 7.9 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. An insulation test showed a winding fault, so the motor was replaced with an exchange unit.
The well stayed shut in for about 17 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part was back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Winding breakdown from heat and age.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 8 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Disconnected and removed the motor.
2. Fitted the exchange motor on the existing base.
3. Aligned the sheaves and tensioned the belts.
4. Checked rotation direction and motor current.

Summary of the fix: replaced the electric motor with an exchange unit.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 2 hours; motor current within the nameplate rating and stroke rate normal. The well was returned to production.
