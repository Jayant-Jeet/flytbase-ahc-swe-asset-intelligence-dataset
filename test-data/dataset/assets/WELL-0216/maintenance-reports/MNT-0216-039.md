## What happened

The weekly drone inspection INS-0216-0219 at 07:00 on 2027-01-14 found crude oil on the pad: about 2.4% of the pad area, estimated 0.5 bbl. The inspector noted: "Pooled fluid near flowline." The field operator shut the well in, closed the flowline valve and placed earth berms and absorbent pads. The release was contained about 3.4 hours after the inspection. The crew traced the leak to a threaded coupling on the flowline close to the wellhead.

## Root cause

Corrosion under a failed protective wrap on the coupling.
This is corrective repair number 5 on this well's flowline in the last 12 months.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 3 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).
- Hot work permit issued for welding; gas test before welding read 3% LEL, below the 10% limit (WS-04).

## Work done so far

1. Excavated around the coupling.
2. Cut out the failed coupling and the pipe ends.
3. Welded in new pipe and a new fitting.

Still to do: complete the remaining repair steps, test the equipment and return the well to production.

Summary of the fix: replaced leaking flowline section and remediated soil.

## Testing and return to service
