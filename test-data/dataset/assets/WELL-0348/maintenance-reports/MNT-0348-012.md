# Maintenance Report — MNT-0348-012

**Asset:** WELL-0348 (NW-12 Well 348, T200, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 28 Oct 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 07 Nov 2023 05:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 237 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster, Kevin Price
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0348-0054 on 2023-10-26
**Report written:** 13 Nov 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that the pumper heard grinding from the walking beam and saw the horsehead wobbling. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0348-0054 showed the wellhead at 24.6 °C with the air at 23.0 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. A vibration check and visual inspection found play in the saddle bearing and scoring on the bearing housing.
The well stayed shut in for about 8 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Bearing wear from long service and an overdue grease interval.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Lowered the horsehead and supported the walking beam with a crane.
2. Removed the saddle bearing assembly.
3. Cleaned the housing and checked alignment.
4. Fitted a new saddle bearing and torqued the bolts to the manufacturer's values.
5. Greased all structural bearings.

Summary of the fix: replaced saddle bearing and re-greased.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 2 hours: smooth stroke, no knocking, bearing temperature normal. The well was returned to production.
