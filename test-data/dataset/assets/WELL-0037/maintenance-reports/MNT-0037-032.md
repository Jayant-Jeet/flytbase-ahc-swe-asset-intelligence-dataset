# Maintenance Report — MNT-0037-032

**Asset:** WELL-0037 (SW-23 Well 037, T200, Southwest Block 23)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 01 Jan 2027 08:00 · **Finished:** 12 Jan 2027 13:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 269 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Megan Ward · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0037-0216 on 2026-12-31
**Report written:** 13 Jan 2027

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0037-0216 showed the wellhead at 12.6 °C with the air at 8.1 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 9 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Paraffin build-up stopped the float from moving.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 5 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
