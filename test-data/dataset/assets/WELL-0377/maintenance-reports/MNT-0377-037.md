## What happened

Field staff reported that the motor breaker kept tripping and the pump jack would not run. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0377-0219 showed the wellhead at 7.9 °C with the air at 7.2 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. An insulation test showed a winding fault, so the motor was replaced with an exchange unit.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 15 days longer than the repair itself because the job has to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Moisture got into the motor terminal box and damaged the windings.
