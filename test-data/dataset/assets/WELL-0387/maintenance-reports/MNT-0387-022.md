# Maintenance Report — MNT-0387-022

**Asset:** WELL-0387 (NW-12 Well 387, T500, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 11 Jul 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 29 Jul 2025 16:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 440 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Megan Ward, Daniel Foster
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0387-0138 on 2025-07-10
**Report written:** 30 Jul 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator was overfilling and the dump valve was not opening. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0387-0138 showed the wellhead at 45.9 °C with the air at 37.3 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 16 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part was back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Controller float and switch failure.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
