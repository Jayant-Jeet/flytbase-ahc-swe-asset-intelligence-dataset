# Maintenance Report — MNT-0519-015

**Asset:** WELL-0519 (SE-32 Well 519, T500, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 27 Apr 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 28 Apr 2025 20:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 36 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0519-0130 on 2025-04-24
**Report written:** 29 Apr 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0519-0130 showed the wellhead at 39.3 °C with the air at 27.1 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.

## Root cause

Controller float and switch failure.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 6 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
