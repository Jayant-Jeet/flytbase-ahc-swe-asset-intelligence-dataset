## What happened

Field staff reported that the operator noticed the pump jack running unusually light and no fluid reaching the flowline. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0093-0216 showed the wellhead at 10.6 °C with the air at 6.1 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. A dynamometer card showed no load from the downhole pump, which points to a parted rod.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 9 days longer than the repair itself because the workover rig is busy on other wells.

## Root cause

Fatigue failure of a rod body, probably started by corrosion pitting.

- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).
