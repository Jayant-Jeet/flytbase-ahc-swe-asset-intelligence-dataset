# Maintenance Report — MNT-0290-004

**Asset:** WELL-0290 (NE-08 Well 290, T200, Northeast Block 8)
**Equipment:** wellhead
**Work started:** 19 May 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 24 May 2023 18:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 130 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman, Megan Ward · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0290-0031 on 2023-05-18
**Report written:** 25 May 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that fluid was weeping from the stuffing box around the polished rod. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0290-0031 showed the wellhead at 34.7 °C with the air at 33.2 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The packing rings were worn and the polished rod was lightly scored.
The well stayed shut in for about 3 days longer than the repair itself because the crew was committed to a higher-priority spill response elsewhere in the field.

## Root cause

Normal packing wear.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Closed the wellhead valves and bled off pressure.
2. Removed the old packing.
3. Installed new packing rings and adjusted the gland.
4. Cleaned the wellhead area.

Summary of the fix: replaced stuffing box packing.

## Testing and return to service

Checked the stuffing box with the unit running: no leak. The well was returned to production.
