## What happened

Field staff reported that oil was dripping from the gearbox onto the pump jack base. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0528-0218 showed the wellhead at 11.6 °C with the air at 5.9 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The input shaft seal was worn and the gearbox oil level was below the sight glass.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 21 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part is back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Seal wear, made worse by heat and a low oil level.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 5 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done so far

1. Drained the gearbox oil into a closed container.
2. Replaced the input and output shaft seals.

Still to do: complete the remaining repair steps, test the equipment and return the well to production.

Summary of the fix: replaced gearbox seals and topped up oil.
