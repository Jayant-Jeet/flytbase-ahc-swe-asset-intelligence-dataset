# Maintenance Report — MNT-0528-003

**Asset:** WELL-0528 (SE-32 Well 528, T200, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** wellhead
**Work started:** 27 Jan 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 06 Feb 2023 23:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 255 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Megan Ward, Kevin Price
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0528-0015 on 2023-01-26
**Report written:** 07 Feb 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that fluid was weeping from the stuffing box around the polished rod. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0528-0015 showed the wellhead at 14.0 °C with the air at 9.9 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The packing rings were worn and the polished rod was lightly scored.
The well stayed shut in for about 9 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Normal packing wear.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 2 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Closed the wellhead valves and bled off pressure.
2. Removed the old packing.
3. Installed new packing rings and adjusted the gland.
4. Cleaned the wellhead area.

Summary of the fix: replaced stuffing box packing.

## Testing and return to service

Checked the stuffing box with the unit running: no leak. The well was returned to production.
