# Maintenance Report — MNT-0344-005

**Asset:** WELL-0344 (NW-12 Well 344, T500, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** wellhead
**Work started:** 30 Jun 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 05 Jul 2023 20:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 132 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0344-0036 on 2023-06-29
**Report written:** 06 Jul 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that fluid was weeping around the ESP power cable penetrator on the wellhead. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0344-0036 showed the wellhead at 53.9 °C with the air at 37.9 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The penetrator seal had hardened and no longer sealed around the power cable.
The well stayed shut in for about 4 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part was back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Seal damaged when the cable was last re-terminated.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Stopped the ESP drive and isolated the well.
2. Bled off wellhead pressure.
3. Replaced the penetrator seal kit.
4. Pressure tested the wellhead.
5. Restarted the drive.

Summary of the fix: replaced the cable penetrator seal.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure test passed and no weeping with the ESP running. The well was returned to production.
