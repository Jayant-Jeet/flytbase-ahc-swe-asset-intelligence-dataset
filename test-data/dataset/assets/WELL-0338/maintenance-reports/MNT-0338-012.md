# Maintenance Report — MNT-0338-012

**Asset:** WELL-0338 (NW-12 Well 338, T200, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 18 Aug 2024 08:00 · **Finished:** 10 Sep 2024 13:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 557 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman, Megan Ward · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0338-0092 on 2024-08-15
**Report written:** 10 Sep 2024

## What happened

Field staff reported that the operator found an oil puddle under the gearbox. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0338-0092 showed the wellhead at 34.1 °C with the air at 30.6 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The input shaft seal was worn and the gearbox oil level was below the sight glass.
The well stayed shut in for about 21 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

A hardened output shaft seal after years of service.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Drained the gearbox oil into a closed container.
2. Replaced the input and output shaft seals.
3. Cleaned the base and absorbed the spilled oil.
4. Refilled with the specified gear oil to the correct level.

Summary of the fix: replaced gearbox seals and topped up oil.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 1 hour and checked both shafts for leaks: none found. The well was returned to production.

## Waste

- Used gear oil and oily pads sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0338-012 (WS-06).
