# Maintenance Report — MNT-0354-001

**Asset:** WELL-0354 (NW-12 Well 354, T200, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 02 Dec 2022 08:00 · **Finished:** 13 Dec 2022 12:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 268 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Kevin Price · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0354-0007 on 2022-12-01
**Report written:** 14 Dec 2022

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0354-0007 showed the wellhead at 10.5 °C with the air at 8.1 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 9 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Controller float and switch failure.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
