# Maintenance Report — MNT-0244-020

**Asset:** WELL-0244 (SE-31 Well 244, T200, Southeast Block 31)
**Equipment:** rod string
**Work started:** 22 Feb 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 02 Mar 2026 21:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 205 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0244-0147 on 2026-02-19
**Report written:** 02 Mar 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the pump jack was running but the wellhead was cold and the rod load had dropped. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0244-0147 showed the wellhead at 9.9 °C with the air at 7.8 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. A dynamometer card showed no load from the downhole pump, which points to a parted rod.
The company workover rig was busy on other wells, so the well waited about 7 days for it; the work itself took about 2 days.

## Root cause

A coupling unscrewed after repeated rod buckling at the bottom of the stroke.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Rigged up the company workover rig on the well.
2. Pulled the rod string and located the break.
3. Recovered the lower section with an overshot fishing tool.
4. Replaced the damaged rods.
5. Ran the rod string back in and spaced out the pump.

Summary of the fix: fished and replaced parted rod section.

## Testing and return to service

Pump test showed normal fluid delivery and the rod load returned to normal. The well was returned to production.
