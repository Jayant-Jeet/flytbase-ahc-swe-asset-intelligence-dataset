## What happened

Field staff reported that the inspector saw a thin film of oil on the wellhead. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0408-0218 showed the wellhead at 17.7 °C with the air at 10.6 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The packing rings were worn and the polished rod was lightly scored.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 2 days longer than the repair itself because the crew is committed to a higher-priority spill response elsewhere in the field.

## Root cause

Normal packing wear.
