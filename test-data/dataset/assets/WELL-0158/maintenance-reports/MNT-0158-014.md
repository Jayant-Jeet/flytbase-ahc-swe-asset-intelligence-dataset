# Maintenance Report — MNT-0158-014

**Asset:** WELL-0158 (NE-08 Well 158, T350, Northeast Block 8)
**Equipment:** wellhead
**Work started:** 28 Mar 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 02 Apr 2025 05:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 117 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman, Kevin Price
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0158-0126 on 2025-03-27
**Report written:** 03 Apr 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the inspector saw a thin film of oil on the wellhead. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0158-0126 showed the wellhead at 25.4 °C with the air at 15.0 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The packing rings were worn and the polished rod was lightly scored.
The well stayed shut in for about 3 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Normal packing wear.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 4 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Closed the wellhead valves and bled off pressure.
2. Removed the old packing.
3. Installed new packing rings and adjusted the gland.
4. Cleaned the wellhead area.

Summary of the fix: replaced stuffing box packing.

## Testing and return to service

Checked the stuffing box with the unit running: no leak. The well was returned to production.
