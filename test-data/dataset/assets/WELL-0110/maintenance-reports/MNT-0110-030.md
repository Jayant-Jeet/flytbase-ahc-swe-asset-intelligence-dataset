# Maintenance Report — MNT-0110-030

**Asset:** WELL-0110 (NE-07 Well 110, T200, Northeast Block 7)
**Equipment:** flowline
**Work started:** 16 Oct 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 27 Oct 2026 01:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 257 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Megan Ward, Brian Coleman
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0110-0205 on 2026-10-15
**Report written:** 02 Nov 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the inspector saw wet soil above the buried flowline. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0110-0205 showed the wellhead at 25.0 °C with the air at 24.7 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Excavation found a corrosion pinhole on the bottom of the line.
The well stayed shut in for about 9 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Internal corrosion from produced water collecting at a low point.
This is corrective repair number 4 on this well's flowline in the last 12 months.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- **Work stopped for H2S:** a personal monitor read 13 ppm, above the 10 ppm stop-work level. The crew moved upwind and waited about 55 minutes until readings were below 10 ppm and the HSE officer approved the restart (WS-03).
- Hot work permit issued for welding; gas test before welding read 0% LEL, below the 10% limit (WS-04).

## Work done

1. Excavated around the leak.
2. Cut out the corroded section.
3. Welded in a new section of line pipe.
4. Coated the new joint.
5. Backfilled after the pressure test.

Summary of the fix: cut out and replaced the corroded flowline section.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure-tested the repaired section at 216 psi (1.25 × the flowline's maximum operating pressure of 173 psi) and held it for 4 hours with no pressure drop (WS-05). The well was returned to production.

## Waste

- Oily soil from the excavation and the cut-out pipe sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0110-030 (WS-06).
