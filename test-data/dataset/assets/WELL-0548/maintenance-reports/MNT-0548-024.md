# Maintenance Report — MNT-0548-024

**Asset:** WELL-0548 (SE-32 Well 548, T500, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 25 Jul 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 05 Aug 2026 21:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 277 hours

**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0548-0190 on 2026-07-23
**Report written:** 06 Aug 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator was overfilling and the dump valve was not opening. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0548-0190 showed the wellhead at 39.9 °C with the air at 34.6 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 10 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Controller float and switch failure.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
