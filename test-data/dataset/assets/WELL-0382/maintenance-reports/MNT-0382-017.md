# Maintenance Report — MNT-0382-017

**Asset:** WELL-0382 (NW-12 Well 382, T500, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** wellhead
**Work started:** 01 Mar 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 04 Mar 2025 04:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 68 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman, Megan Ward
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0382-0120 on 2025-02-27
**Report written:** 05 Mar 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the inspector saw an oil film on the wellhead around the cable entry. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0382-0120 showed the wellhead at 30.4 °C with the air at 15.1 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The penetrator seal had hardened and no longer sealed around the power cable.

## Root cause

Seal ageing from heat cycling.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 2 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Stopped the ESP drive and isolated the well.
2. Bled off wellhead pressure.
3. Replaced the penetrator seal kit.
4. Pressure tested the wellhead.
5. Restarted the drive.

Summary of the fix: replaced the cable penetrator seal.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure test passed and no weeping with the ESP running. The well was returned to production.
