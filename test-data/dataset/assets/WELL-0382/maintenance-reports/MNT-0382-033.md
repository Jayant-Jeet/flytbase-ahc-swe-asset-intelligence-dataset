# Maintenance Report — MNT-0382-033

**Asset:** WELL-0382 (NW-12 Well 382, T500, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** esp pump
**Work started:** 19 Dec 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 30 Dec 2026 03:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 259 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Megan Ward
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0382-0212 on 2026-12-17
**Report written:** 31 Dec 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the ESP drive reported a ground fault. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0382-0212 showed the wellhead at 18.7 °C with the air at 12.9 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Insulation testing located a fault in the downhole power cable.
The company workover rig was busy on other wells, so the well waited about 9 days for it; the work itself took about 2 days.

## Root cause

Cable crushed against the tubing during the last installation.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

**Follow-up:** Crew to return within 30 days to trend the ESP motor current and insulation readings.

## Work done

1. Pulled the ESP string with the workover rig.
2. Located and cut out the damaged cable section.
3. Spliced in new cable and tested insulation.
4. Ran the string back in.
5. Restarted the drive.

Summary of the fix: replaced the damaged ESP power cable section.

## Testing and return to service

Insulation test passed and the ESP ran normally for 4 hours. The well was returned to production.
