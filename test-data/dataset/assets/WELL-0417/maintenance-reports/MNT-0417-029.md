## What happened

The weekly drone inspection INS-0417-0219 at 14:15 on 2027-01-14 found crude oil on the pad: about 7.0% of the pad area, estimated 1.3 bbl. The inspector noted: "Staining along flowline route." The field operator shut the well in, closed the flowline valve and placed earth berms and absorbent pads. The release was contained about 5.5 hours after the inspection. The crew traced the leak to a corrosion hole along the flowline route.

## Root cause

External corrosion where the pipe coating had been damaged.
