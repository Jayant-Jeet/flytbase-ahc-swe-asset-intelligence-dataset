# Maintenance Report — MNT-0307-029

**Asset:** WELL-0307 (NW-12 Well 307, T200, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 10 Oct 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 04 Nov 2026 08:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 600 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0307-0206 on 2026-10-08
**Report written:** 04 Nov 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the electric motor driving the pump jack had stopped and would not restart. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0307-0206 showed the wellhead at 27.9 °C with the air at 23.3 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. An insulation test showed a winding fault, so the motor was replaced with an exchange unit.
The well stayed shut in for about 23 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part was back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Moisture got into the motor terminal box and damaged the windings.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- **Work stopped for H2S:** a personal monitor read 17 ppm, above the 10 ppm stop-work level. The crew moved upwind and waited about 40 minutes until readings were below 10 ppm and the HSE officer approved the restart (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Disconnected and removed the motor.
2. Fitted the exchange motor on the existing base.
3. Aligned the sheaves and tensioned the belts.
4. Checked rotation direction and motor current.

Summary of the fix: replaced the electric motor with an exchange unit.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 2 hours; motor current within the nameplate rating and stroke rate normal. The well was returned to production.
