# Maintenance Report — MNT-0587-026

**Asset:** WELL-0587 (SE-32 Well 587, T350, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 02 May 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 09 Jun 2026 02:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 906 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Kevin Price
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0587-0181 on 2026-04-30
**Report written:** 10 Jun 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0587-0181 showed the wellhead at 35.4 °C with the air at 26.3 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 36 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Controller float and switch failure.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
