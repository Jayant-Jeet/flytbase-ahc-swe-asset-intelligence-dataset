# Maintenance Report — MNT-0473-006

**Asset:** WELL-0473 (SE-32 Well 473, T200, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 26 Nov 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 28 Nov 2023 18:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 58 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Kevin Price, Megan Ward
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0473-0056 on 2023-11-23
**Report written:** 29 Nov 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that the operator found an oil puddle under the gearbox. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0473-0056 showed the wellhead at 15.0 °C with the air at 10.3 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The input shaft seal was worn and the gearbox oil level was below the sight glass.

## Root cause

Seal wear, made worse by heat and a low oil level.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Drained the gearbox oil into a closed container.
2. Replaced the input and output shaft seals.
3. Cleaned the base and absorbed the spilled oil.
4. Refilled with the specified gear oil to the correct level.

Summary of the fix: replaced gearbox seals and topped up oil.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 1 hour and checked both shafts for leaks: none found. The well was returned to production.

## Waste

- Used gear oil and oily pads sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0473-006 (WS-06).
