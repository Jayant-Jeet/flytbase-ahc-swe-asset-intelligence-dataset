# Maintenance Report — MNT-0235-007

**Asset:** WELL-0235 (SE-31 Well 235, T500, Southeast Block 31)
**Equipment:** flowline
**Work started:** 03 Sep 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 20 Sep 2023 15:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 415 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Megan Ward · **Contractor:** Tumbleweed Flowline Co.
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0235-0046 on 2023-08-31
**Report written:** 21 Sep 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that a damp, dark patch appeared along the flowline route. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0235-0046 showed the wellhead at 38.0 °C with the air at 34.8 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Excavation found a corrosion pinhole on the bottom of the line.
The well stayed shut in for about 15 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part was back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

External corrosion where the pipe coating had been damaged.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 2 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).
- Hot work permit issued for welding; gas test before welding read 2% LEL, below the 10% limit (WS-04).

## Work done

1. Excavated around the leak.
2. Cut out the corroded section.
3. Welded in a new section of line pipe.
4. Coated the new joint.
5. Backfilled after the pressure test.

Summary of the fix: cut out and replaced the corroded flowline section.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure-tested the repaired section at 312 psi (1.25 × the flowline's maximum operating pressure of 250 psi) and held it for 4 hours with no pressure drop (WS-05). The well was returned to production.

## Waste

- Oily soil from the excavation and the cut-out pipe sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0235-007 (WS-06).
