# Maintenance Report — MNT-0287-003

**Asset:** WELL-0287 (SW-23 Well 287, T500, Southwest Block 23)
**Equipment:** flowline
**Work started:** 31 Mar 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 01 Apr 2023 12:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 28 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman · **Contractor:** Tumbleweed Flowline Co.
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0287-0024 on 2023-03-30
**Report written:** 03 Apr 2023

## What happened

The weekly drone inspection INS-0287-0024 at 08:30 on 2023-03-30 found crude oil on the pad: about 10.8% of the pad area, estimated 6.2 bbl. The inspector noted: "Staining along flowline route." The field operator shut the well in, closed the flowline valve and placed earth berms and absorbent pads. The release was contained about 1.8 hours after the inspection. The crew traced the leak to a corrosion hole along the flowline route.

## Root cause

External corrosion where the pipe coating had been damaged.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 2 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).
- Hot work permit issued for welding; gas test before welding read 1% LEL, below the 10% limit (WS-04).

## Work done

1. Excavated around the leak point.
2. Cut out the corroded section.
3. Welded in new line pipe.
4. Coated the new joints.
5. Excavated the stained soil.
6. Backfilled after the pressure test.

Summary of the fix: replaced leaking flowline section and remediated soil.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure-tested the repaired section at 242 psi (1.25 × the flowline's maximum operating pressure of 194 psi) and held it for 4 hours with no pressure drop (WS-05). The well was returned to production.

## Spill clean-up and waste

- About 3.8 m³ of stained soil excavated, bagged and sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0287-003 (WS-06).
- 4 soil samples taken around the spill; the highest result after clean-up was 0.82% TPH, below the 1.0% target (SR-03).
