# Maintenance Report — MNT-0083-010

**Asset:** WELL-0083 (SW-24 Well 083, T200, Southwest Block 24)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 08 Oct 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 24 Oct 2023 20:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 396 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Kevin Price, Brian Coleman · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0083-0051 on 2023-10-05
**Report written:** 25 Oct 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that oil was dripping from the gearbox onto the pump jack base. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0083-0051 showed the wellhead at 26.1 °C with the air at 26.3 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The input shaft seal was worn and the gearbox oil level was below the sight glass.
The well stayed shut in for about 14 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part was back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

A hardened output shaft seal after years of service.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level; one crew member's monitor had not been bump-tested that day (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Drained the gearbox oil into a closed container.
2. Replaced the input and output shaft seals.
3. Cleaned the base and absorbed the spilled oil.
4. Refilled with the specified gear oil to the correct level.

Summary of the fix: replaced gearbox seals and topped up oil.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 1 hour and checked both shafts for leaks: none found. The well was returned to production.

## Waste

- Used gear oil and oily pads sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0083-010 (WS-06).
