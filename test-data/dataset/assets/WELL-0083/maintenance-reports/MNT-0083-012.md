# Maintenance Report — MNT-0083-012

**Asset:** WELL-0083 (SW-24 Well 083, T200, Southwest Block 24)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 01 Dec 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 09 Jan 2024 21:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 949 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Kevin Price, Brian Coleman · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0083-0059 on 2023-11-30
**Report written:** 10 Jan 2024

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0083-0059 showed the wellhead at 8.9 °C with the air at 9.2 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 38 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Paraffin build-up stopped the float from moving.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
