# Maintenance Report — MNT-0556-003

**Asset:** WELL-0556 (SE-32 Well 556, T500, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** esp pump
**Work started:** 27 May 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 31 May 2023 00:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 88 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman, Kevin Price · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0556-0030 on 2023-05-25
**Report written:** 01 Jun 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that the ESP drive reported a ground fault. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0556-0030 showed the wellhead at 38.1 °C with the air at 30.3 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Insulation testing located a fault in the downhole power cable.
Coyote Ridge Well Services's workover rig was busy on other wells, so the well waited about 2 days for it; the work itself took about 2 days.

## Root cause

Cable insulation damaged by heat and gas.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level; one crew member's monitor had not been bump-tested that day (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Pulled the ESP string with the workover rig.
2. Located and cut out the damaged cable section.
3. Spliced in new cable and tested insulation.
4. Ran the string back in.
5. Restarted the drive.

Summary of the fix: replaced the damaged ESP power cable section.

## Testing and return to service

Insulation test passed and the ESP ran normally for 4 hours. The well was returned to production.
