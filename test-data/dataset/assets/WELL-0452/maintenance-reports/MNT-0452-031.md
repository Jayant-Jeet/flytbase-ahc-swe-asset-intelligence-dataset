## What happened

Field staff reported that fluid stopped reaching the flowline and the rod load spiked on the downstroke. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0452-0217 showed the wellhead at 10.7 °C with the air at 11.5 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The downhole pump was stuck, most likely from sand or scale.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 36 days longer than the repair itself because the workover rig is busy on other wells.

## Root cause

Sand and scale build-up in the pump barrel.

- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 4 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).
