# Maintenance Report — MNT-0027-024

**Asset:** WELL-0027 (SW-23 Well 027, T200, Southwest Block 23)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 10 Oct 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 22 Oct 2025 08:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 288 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0027-0154 on 2025-10-09
**Report written:** 23 Oct 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0027-0154 showed the wellhead at 19.1 °C with the air at 19.7 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 10 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Controller float and switch failure.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 4 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
