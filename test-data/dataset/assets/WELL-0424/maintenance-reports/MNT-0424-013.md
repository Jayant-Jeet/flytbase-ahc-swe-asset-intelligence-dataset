# Maintenance Report — MNT-0424-013

**Asset:** WELL-0424 (NW-12 Well 424, T200, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 10 Aug 2024 08:00 · **Finished:** 28 Aug 2024 14:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 438 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Kevin Price, Daniel Foster
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0424-0093 on 2024-08-08
**Report written:** 28 Aug 2024

## What happened

Field staff reported that the motor breaker kept tripping and the pump jack would not run. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0424-0093 showed the wellhead at 42.0 °C with the air at 38.2 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. An insulation test showed a winding fault, so the motor was replaced with an exchange unit.
The well stayed shut in for about 16 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Moisture got into the motor terminal box and damaged the windings.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Disconnected and removed the motor.
2. Fitted the exchange motor on the existing base.
3. Aligned the sheaves and tensioned the belts.
4. Checked rotation direction and motor current.

Summary of the fix: replaced the electric motor with an exchange unit.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 2 hours; motor current within the nameplate rating and stroke rate normal. The well was returned to production.
