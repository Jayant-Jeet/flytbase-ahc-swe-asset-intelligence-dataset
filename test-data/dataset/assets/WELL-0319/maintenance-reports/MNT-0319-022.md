# Maintenance Report — MNT-0319-022

**Asset:** WELL-0319 (NW-12 Well 319, T500, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** esp pump
**Work started:** 06 Apr 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 16 Apr 2025 21:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 253 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0319-0127 on 2025-04-03
**Report written:** 17 Apr 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the ESP kept tripping on start-up. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0319-0127 showed the wellhead at 29.7 °C with the air at 20.7 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Insulation testing located a fault in the downhole power cable.
Coyote Ridge Well Services's workover rig was busy on other wells, so the well waited about 9 days for it; the work itself took about 2 days.

## Root cause

Cable crushed against the tubing during the last installation.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 3 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Pulled the ESP string with the workover rig.
2. Located and cut out the damaged cable section.
3. Spliced in new cable and tested insulation.
4. Ran the string back in.
5. Restarted the drive.

Summary of the fix: replaced the damaged ESP power cable section.

## Testing and return to service

Insulation test passed and the ESP ran normally for 4 hours. The well was returned to production.
