# Maintenance Report — MNT-0546-013

**Asset:** WELL-0546 (SE-32 Well 546, T500, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** wellhead
**Work started:** 26 May 2024 08:00 · **Finished:** 29 May 2024 15:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 79 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman, Daniel Foster · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0546-0083 on 2024-05-23
**Report written:** 30 May 2024

## What happened

Field staff reported that fluid was weeping around the ESP power cable penetrator on the wellhead. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0546-0083 showed the wellhead at 34.1 °C with the air at 34.5 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The penetrator seal had hardened and no longer sealed around the power cable.
The well stayed shut in for about 1 day longer than the repair itself because the crew was committed to a higher-priority spill response elsewhere in the field.

## Root cause

Seal ageing from heat cycling.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Stopped the ESP drive and isolated the well.
2. Bled off wellhead pressure.
3. Replaced the penetrator seal kit.
4. Pressure tested the wellhead.
5. Restarted the drive.

Summary of the fix: replaced the cable penetrator seal.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure test passed and no weeping with the ESP running. The well was returned to production.
