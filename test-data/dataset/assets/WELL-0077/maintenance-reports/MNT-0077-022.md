# Maintenance Report — MNT-0077-022

**Asset:** WELL-0077 (SW-24 Well 077, T200, Southwest Block 24)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 13 Jun 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 16 Jun 2026 04:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 68 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Megan Ward · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0077-0183 on 2026-06-11
**Report written:** 17 Jun 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0077-0183 showed the wellhead at 31.3 °C with the air at 31.7 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.

## Root cause

Controller float and switch failure.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
