# Maintenance Report — MNT-0135-023

**Asset:** WELL-0135 (NE-07 Well 135, T200, Northeast Block 7)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 03 Aug 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 06 Aug 2025 02:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 66 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0135-0145 on 2025-07-31
**Report written:** 06 Aug 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the electric motor driving the pump jack had stopped and would not restart. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0135-0145 showed the wellhead at 34.4 °C with the air at 32.2 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. An insulation test showed a winding fault, so the motor was replaced with an exchange unit.

## Root cause

Moisture got into the motor terminal box and damaged the windings.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started, but it was not written down (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 8 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Disconnected and removed the motor.
2. Fitted the exchange motor on the existing base.
3. Aligned the sheaves and tensioned the belts.
4. Checked rotation direction and motor current.

Summary of the fix: replaced the electric motor with an exchange unit.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 2 hours; motor current within the nameplate rating and stroke rate normal. The well was returned to production.
