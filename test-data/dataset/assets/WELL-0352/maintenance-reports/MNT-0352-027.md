# Maintenance Report — MNT-0352-027

**Asset:** WELL-0352 (NW-12 Well 352, T500, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** esp pump
**Work started:** 02 Oct 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 13 Oct 2026 15:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 271 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Kevin Price
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0352-0204 on 2026-10-01
**Report written:** 13 Oct 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the ESP kept tripping on start-up. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0352-0204 showed the wellhead at 27.8 °C with the air at 24.9 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Insulation testing located a fault in the downhole power cable.
The company workover rig was busy on other wells, so the well waited about 9 days for it; the work itself took about 2 days.

## Root cause

Cable crushed against the tubing during the last installation.
This is corrective repair number 3 on this well's esp pump in the last 12 months.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started, but it was not written down (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Pulled the ESP string with the workover rig.
2. Located and cut out the damaged cable section.
3. Spliced in new cable and tested insulation.
4. Ran the string back in.
5. Restarted the drive.

Summary of the fix: replaced the damaged ESP power cable section.

## Testing and return to service

Insulation test passed and the ESP ran normally for 4 hours. The well was returned to production.
