# Maintenance Report — MNT-0578-019

**Asset:** WELL-0578 (SE-32 Well 578, T500, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 11 May 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 21 May 2025 03:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 235 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Megan Ward, Daniel Foster · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0578-0133 on 2025-05-08
**Report written:** 22 May 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0578-0133 showed the wellhead at 35.4 °C with the air at 25.3 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 8 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Controller float and switch failure.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 4 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
