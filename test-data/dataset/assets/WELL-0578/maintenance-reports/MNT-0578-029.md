# Maintenance Report — MNT-0578-029

**Asset:** WELL-0578 (SE-32 Well 578, T500, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 13 Sep 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 30 Sep 2026 11:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 411 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0578-0202 on 2026-09-10
**Report written:** 02 Oct 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0578-0202 showed the wellhead at 44.1 °C with the air at 33.4 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 15 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Paraffin build-up stopped the float from moving.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 8 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
