# Maintenance Report — MNT-0343-007

**Asset:** WELL-0343 (NW-12 Well 343, T200, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** wellhead
**Work started:** 01 Jul 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 11 Jul 2023 03:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 235 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Megan Ward
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0343-0036 on 2023-06-29
**Report written:** 17 Jul 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that fluid was weeping from the stuffing box around the polished rod. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0343-0036 showed the wellhead at 36.2 °C with the air at 34.7 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The packing rings were worn and the polished rod was lightly scored.
The well stayed shut in for about 8 days longer than the repair itself because the crew was committed to a higher-priority spill response elsewhere in the field.

## Root cause

Packing wear sped up by a slightly misaligned polished rod.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 2 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Closed the wellhead valves and bled off pressure.
2. Removed the old packing.
3. Installed new packing rings and adjusted the gland.
4. Cleaned the wellhead area.

Summary of the fix: replaced stuffing box packing.

## Testing and return to service

Checked the stuffing box with the unit running: no leak. The well was returned to production.
