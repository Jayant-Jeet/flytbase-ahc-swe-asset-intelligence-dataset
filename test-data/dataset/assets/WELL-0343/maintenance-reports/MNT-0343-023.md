# Maintenance Report — MNT-0343-023

**Asset:** WELL-0343 (NW-12 Well 343, T200, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** wellhead
**Work started:** 13 Sep 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 08 Oct 2025 04:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 596 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Megan Ward, Kevin Price · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0343-0147 on 2025-09-11
**Report written:** 08 Oct 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that fluid was weeping from the stuffing box around the polished rod. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0343-0147 showed the wellhead at 32.7 °C with the air at 27.2 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The packing rings were worn and the polished rod was lightly scored.
The well stayed shut in for about 23 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Normal packing wear.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 2 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Closed the wellhead valves and bled off pressure.
2. Removed the old packing.
3. Installed new packing rings and adjusted the gland.
4. Cleaned the wellhead area.

Summary of the fix: replaced stuffing box packing.

## Testing and return to service

Checked the stuffing box with the unit running: no leak. The well was returned to production.
