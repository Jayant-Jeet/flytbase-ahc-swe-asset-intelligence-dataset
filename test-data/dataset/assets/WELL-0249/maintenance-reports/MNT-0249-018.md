## What happened

Field staff reported that the ESP drive tripped on motor overcurrent and would not restart. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0249-0119 showed the wellhead at 7.8 °C with the air at 5.2 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. An insulation-resistance test from surface showed the downhole ESP motor windings had failed.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 22 days longer than the repair itself because the workover rig is busy on other wells.

## Root cause

Motor insulation breakdown with age and heat.
This is corrective repair number 3 on this well's esp pump in the last 12 months.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 5 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

Still to do: complete the remaining repair steps, test the equipment and return the well to production.

Summary of the fix: pulled the ESP and replaced the motor.
