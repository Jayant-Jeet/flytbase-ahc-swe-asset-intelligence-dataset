# Maintenance Report — MNT-0023-010

**Asset:** WELL-0023 (SW-23 Well 023, T200, Southwest Block 23)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 01 Mar 2024 08:00 · **Finished:** 27 Mar 2024 18:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 634 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Megan Ward, Brian Coleman · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0023-0072 on 2024-02-29
**Report written:** 29 Mar 2024

## What happened

Field staff reported that the operator found an oil puddle under the gearbox. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0023-0072 showed the wellhead at 11.3 °C with the air at 11.8 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The input shaft seal was worn and the gearbox oil level was below the sight glass.
The well stayed shut in for about 24 days longer than the repair itself because the crew was committed to a higher-priority spill response elsewhere in the field.

## Root cause

A hardened output shaft seal after years of service.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Drained the gearbox oil into a closed container.
2. Replaced the input and output shaft seals.
3. Cleaned the base and absorbed the spilled oil.
4. Refilled with the specified gear oil to the correct level.

Summary of the fix: replaced gearbox seals and topped up oil.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 1 hour and checked both shafts for leaks: none found. The well was returned to production.

## Waste

- Used gear oil and oily pads sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0023-010 (WS-06).
