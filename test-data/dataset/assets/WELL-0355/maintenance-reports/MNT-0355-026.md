# Maintenance Report — MNT-0355-026

**Asset:** WELL-0355 (NW-12 Well 355, T200, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** flowline
**Work started:** 11 Sep 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 12 Sep 2026 07:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 23 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman, Daniel Foster · **Contractor:** Tumbleweed Flowline Co.
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0355-0197 on 2026-09-10
**Report written:** 13 Sep 2026

## What happened

The weekly drone inspection INS-0355-0197 at 08:00 on 2026-09-10 found crude oil on the pad: about 10.8% of the pad area, estimated 5.7 bbl. The inspector noted: "Pooled fluid near flowline." The field operator shut the well in, closed the flowline valve and placed earth berms and absorbent pads. The release was contained about 3.5 hours after the inspection. The crew traced the leak to a threaded coupling on the flowline close to the wellhead.

## Root cause

Corrosion under a failed protective wrap on the coupling.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).
- Hot work permit issued for welding; gas test before welding read 0% LEL, below the 10% limit (WS-04).

**Follow-up:** Crew to return within 7 days to check the soil around the repair after the next rain.

## Work done

1. Excavated around the coupling.
2. Cut out the failed coupling and the pipe ends.
3. Welded in new pipe and a new fitting.
4. Coated the new joints.
5. Excavated the stained soil.
6. Backfilled after the pressure test.

Summary of the fix: replaced leaking flowline section and remediated soil.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure-tested the repaired section at 281 psi (1.25 × the flowline's maximum operating pressure of 225 psi) and held it for 4 hours with no pressure drop (WS-05). The well was returned to production.

## Spill clean-up and waste

- About 3.8 m³ of stained soil excavated, bagged and sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0355-026 (WS-06).
- 4 soil samples taken around the spill; the highest result after clean-up was 0.77% TPH, below the 1.0% target (SR-03).
