# Maintenance Report — MNT-0595-031

**Asset:** WELL-0595 (SE-32 Well 595, T200, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** downhole pump
**Work started:** 10 Jan 2027 07:00 · **Finished:** 11 Jan 2027 15:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 32 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Megan Ward, Kevin Price · **Contractor:** Sandstone Rod & Pump
**Triggered by:** operator call-out
**Report written:** 13 Jan 2027

## What happened

Field staff reported that the installed downhole pump came from a lot named in the Highline Pump Co. recall notice of 1 Dec 2026. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The pump was still running normally; it was pulled and replaced as the recall notice requires.

## Root cause

Supplier recall of the pump lot for plunger cracking; the pump had not failed in service.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started, but it was not written down (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 6 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

**Parts installed:** Highline RP-175 pump, serial SN-753918, lot LOT-2503-B

## Work done

1. Rigged up the workover rig on the well.
2. Pulled the rods and the recalled insert pump.
3. Ran in a new insert pump from an unaffected lot.
4. Re-spaced the pump and hung the rods back on.

Summary of the fix: pulled the recalled pump and ran in a new insert pump.

## Testing and return to service

Pump test confirmed fluid to surface and the wellhead warmed back to normal. The well was returned to production.
