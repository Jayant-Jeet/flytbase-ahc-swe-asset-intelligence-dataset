# Maintenance Report — MNT-0013-038

**Asset:** WELL-0013 (SW-23 Well 013, T350, Southwest Block 23)
**Equipment:** flowline
**Work started:** 18 Dec 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 12 Jan 2027 07:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 599 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman, Kevin Price
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0013-0215 on 2026-12-17
**Report written:** 14 Jan 2027

## What happened

Field staff reported that the inspector saw wet soil above the buried flowline. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0013-0215 showed the wellhead at 11.4 °C with the air at 6.5 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Excavation found a corrosion pinhole on the bottom of the line.
The well stayed shut in for about 23 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

External corrosion where the pipe coating had been damaged.
This is corrective repair number 6 on this well's flowline in the last 12 months.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).
- Hot work permit issued for welding; gas test before welding read 3% LEL, below the 10% limit (WS-04).

## Work done

1. Excavated around the leak.
2. Cut out the corroded section.
3. Welded in a new section of line pipe.
4. Coated the new joint.
5. Backfilled after the pressure test.

Summary of the fix: cut out and replaced the corroded flowline section.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure-tested the repaired section at 340 psi (1.25 × the flowline's maximum operating pressure of 272 psi) and held it for 4 hours with no pressure drop (WS-05). The well was returned to production.

## Waste

- Oily soil from the excavation and the cut-out pipe sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0013-038 (WS-06).
