# Maintenance Report — MNT-0456-016

**Asset:** WELL-0456 (SE-32 Well 456, T350, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 06 Dec 2024 08:00 · **Finished:** 24 Dec 2024 08:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 432 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Kevin Price
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0456-0107 on 2024-12-05
**Report written:** 25 Dec 2024

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator was overfilling and the dump valve was not opening. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0456-0107 showed the wellhead at 8.3 °C with the air at 8.3 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 16 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Controller float and switch failure.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
