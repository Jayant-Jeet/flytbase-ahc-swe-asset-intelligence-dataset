# Maintenance Report — MNT-0203-009

**Asset:** WELL-0203 (SE-31 Well 203, T500, Southeast Block 31)
**Equipment:** esp pump
**Work started:** 26 Nov 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 20 Dec 2023 13:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 581 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Megan Ward, Brian Coleman · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0203-0057 on 2023-11-23
**Report written:** 22 Dec 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that the ESP kept tripping on start-up. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0203-0057 showed the wellhead at 16.4 °C with the air at 13.2 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Insulation testing located a fault in the downhole power cable.
Coyote Ridge Well Services's workover rig was busy on other wells, so the well waited about 22 days for it; the work itself took about 2 days.

## Root cause

Cable crushed against the tubing during the last installation.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 4 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Pulled the ESP string with the workover rig.
2. Located and cut out the damaged cable section.
3. Spliced in new cable and tested insulation.
4. Ran the string back in.
5. Restarted the drive.

Summary of the fix: replaced the damaged ESP power cable section.

## Testing and return to service

Insulation test passed and the ESP ran normally for 4 hours. The well was returned to production.
