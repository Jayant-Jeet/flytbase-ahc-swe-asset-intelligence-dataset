# Maintenance Report — MNT-0596-015

**Asset:** WELL-0596 (SE-32 Well 596, T500, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** wellhead
**Work started:** 20 Dec 2024 08:00 · **Finished:** 15 Jan 2025 05:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 621 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Kevin Price
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0596-0112 on 2024-12-19
**Report written:** 15 Jan 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the inspector saw an oil film on the wellhead around the cable entry. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0596-0112 showed the wellhead at 19.3 °C with the air at 13.0 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The penetrator seal had hardened and no longer sealed around the power cable.
The well stayed shut in for about 24 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Seal ageing from heat cycling.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Stopped the ESP drive and isolated the well.
2. Bled off wellhead pressure.
3. Replaced the penetrator seal kit.
4. Pressure tested the wellhead.
5. Restarted the drive.

Summary of the fix: replaced the cable penetrator seal.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure test passed and no weeping with the ESP running. The well was returned to production.
