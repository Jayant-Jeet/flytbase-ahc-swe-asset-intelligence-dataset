# Maintenance Report — MNT-0061-028

**Asset:** WELL-0061 (SW-24 Well 061, T200, Southwest Block 24)
**Equipment:** rod string
**Work started:** 06 Sep 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 30 Sep 2026 08:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 576 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0061-0202 on 2026-09-03
**Report written:** 02 Oct 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the pump jack was running but the wellhead was cold and the rod load had dropped. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0061-0202 showed the wellhead at 38.9 °C with the air at 33.6 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. A dynamometer card showed no load from the downhole pump, which points to a parted rod.
The company workover rig was busy on other wells, so the well waited about 22 days for it; the work itself took about 2 days.

## Root cause

A coupling unscrewed after repeated rod buckling at the bottom of the stroke.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Rigged up the company workover rig on the well.
2. Pulled the rod string and located the break.
3. Recovered the lower section with an overshot fishing tool.
4. Replaced the damaged rods.
5. Ran the rod string back in and spaced out the pump.

Summary of the fix: fished and replaced parted rod section.

## Testing and return to service

Pump test showed normal fluid delivery and the rod load returned to normal. The well was returned to production.
