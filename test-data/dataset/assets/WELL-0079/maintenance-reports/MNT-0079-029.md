# Maintenance Report — MNT-0079-029

**Asset:** WELL-0079 (SW-24 Well 079, T350, Southwest Block 24)
**Equipment:** flowline
**Work started:** 21 Nov 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 16 Dec 2025 02:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 594 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster · **Contractor:** Tumbleweed Flowline Co.
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0079-0155 on 2025-11-20
**Report written:** 17 Dec 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that a damp, dark patch appeared along the flowline route. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0079-0155 showed the wellhead at 21.8 °C with the air at 15.6 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Excavation found a corrosion pinhole on the bottom of the line.
The well stayed shut in for about 23 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Internal corrosion from produced water collecting at a low point.
This is corrective repair number 3 on this well's flowline in the last 12 months.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).
- Hot work permit issued for welding; gas test before welding read 3% LEL, below the 10% limit (WS-04).

## Work done

1. Excavated around the leak.
2. Cut out the corroded section.
3. Welded in a new section of line pipe.
4. Coated the new joint.
5. Backfilled after the pressure test.

Summary of the fix: cut out and replaced the corroded flowline section.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure-tested the repaired section at 391 psi (1.25 × the flowline's maximum operating pressure of 313 psi) and held it for 4 hours with no pressure drop (WS-05). The well was returned to production.

## Waste

- Oily soil from the excavation and the cut-out pipe sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0079-029 (WS-06).
