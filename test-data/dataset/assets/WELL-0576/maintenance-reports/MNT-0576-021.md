# Maintenance Report — MNT-0576-021

**Asset:** WELL-0576 (SE-32 Well 576, T200, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** downhole pump
**Work started:** 20 Nov 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 09 Dec 2026 06:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 454 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Kevin Price, Daniel Foster
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0576-0192 on 2026-11-19
**Report written:** 10 Dec 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that fluid stopped reaching the flowline and the rod load spiked on the downstroke. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0576-0192 showed the wellhead at 18.1 °C with the air at 13.0 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The downhole pump was stuck, most likely from sand or scale.
The company workover rig was busy on other wells, so the well waited about 17 days for it; the work itself took about 2 days.

## Root cause

Free gas entering the pump stopped it from filling, and the dry plunger seized.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- **Work stopped for H2S:** a personal monitor read 13 ppm, above the 10 ppm stop-work level. The crew moved upwind and waited about 70 minutes until readings were below 10 ppm and the HSE officer approved the restart (WS-03).

**Parts installed:** Highline RP-175 pump, serial SN-680982, lot LOT-2506-B

## Work done

1. Rigged up the company workover rig on the well.
2. Pulled the rods and the insert pump.
3. Inspected the pump: sand in the barrel and a worn plunger.
4. Ran in a new insert pump.
5. Re-spaced the pump and hung the rods back on.

Summary of the fix: pulled and replaced downhole pump.

## Testing and return to service

Pump test confirmed fluid to surface and the wellhead warmed back to normal. The well was returned to production.
