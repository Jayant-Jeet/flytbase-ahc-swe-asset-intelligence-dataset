## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator was overfilling and the dump valve was not opening. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0562-0217 showed the wellhead at 12.1 °C with the air at 10.6 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 22 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part is back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Paraffin build-up stopped the float from moving.
