# Maintenance Report — MNT-0394-007

**Asset:** WELL-0394 (NW-12 Well 394, T200, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 09 Jun 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 21 Jun 2023 04:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 284 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0394-0034 on 2023-06-08
**Report written:** 23 Jun 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0394-0034 showed the wellhead at 36.6 °C with the air at 34.5 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 10 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Paraffin build-up stopped the float from moving.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
