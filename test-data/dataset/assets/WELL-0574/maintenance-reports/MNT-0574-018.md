# Maintenance Report — MNT-0574-018

**Asset:** WELL-0574 (SE-32 Well 574, T200, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 25 May 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 28 May 2025 05:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 69 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster, Kevin Price · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0574-0135 on 2025-05-22
**Report written:** 29 May 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that oil was dripping from the gearbox onto the pump jack base. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0574-0135 showed the wellhead at 34.5 °C with the air at 30.8 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The input shaft seal was worn and the gearbox oil level was below the sight glass.

## Root cause

Seal wear, made worse by heat and a low oil level.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Drained the gearbox oil into a closed container.
2. Replaced the input and output shaft seals.
3. Cleaned the base and absorbed the spilled oil.
4. Refilled with the specified gear oil to the correct level.

Summary of the fix: replaced gearbox seals and topped up oil.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 1 hour and checked both shafts for leaks: none found. The well was returned to production.

## Waste

- Used gear oil and oily pads sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0574-018 (WS-06).
