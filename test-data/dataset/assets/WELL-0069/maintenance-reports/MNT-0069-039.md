## What happened

Field staff reported that the electric motor driving the pump jack had stopped and would not restart. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0069-0217 showed the wellhead at 9.0 °C with the air at 4.2 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. An insulation test showed a winding fault, so the motor was replaced with an exchange unit.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 2 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part is back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Winding breakdown from heat and age.
This is corrective repair number 3 on this well's pump jack in the last 12 months.
