# Maintenance Report — MNT-0038-011

**Asset:** WELL-0038 (SW-23 Well 038, T350, Southwest Block 23)
**Equipment:** downhole pump
**Work started:** 06 Jan 2024 08:00 · **Finished:** 10 Jan 2024 17:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 105 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0038-0064 on 2024-01-04
**Report written:** 10 Jan 2024

## What happened

Field staff reported that fluid stopped reaching the flowline and the rod load spiked on the downstroke. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0038-0064 showed the wellhead at 21.2 °C with the air at 12.1 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The downhole pump was stuck, most likely from sand or scale.
The company workover rig was busy on other wells, so the well waited about 2 days for it; the work itself took about 2 days.

## Root cause

Sand and scale build-up in the pump barrel.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 2 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Rigged up the company workover rig on the well.
2. Pulled the rods and the insert pump.
3. Inspected the pump: sand in the barrel and a worn plunger.
4. Ran in a new insert pump.
5. Re-spaced the pump and hung the rods back on.

Summary of the fix: pulled and replaced downhole pump.

## Testing and return to service

Pump test confirmed fluid to surface and the wellhead warmed back to normal. The well was returned to production.
