# Maintenance Report — MNT-0299-027

**Asset:** WELL-0299 (SW-23 Well 299, T500, Southwest Block 23)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 18 Apr 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 13 May 2026 18:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 610 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0299-0179 on 2026-04-16
**Report written:** 15 May 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0299-0179 showed the wellhead at 28.6 °C with the air at 20.3 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 23 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part was back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Controller float and switch failure.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
