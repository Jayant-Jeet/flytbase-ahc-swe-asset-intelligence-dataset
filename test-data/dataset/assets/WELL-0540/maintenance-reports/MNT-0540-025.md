## What happened

Field staff reported that the motor breaker kept tripping and the pump jack would not run. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0540-0214 showed the wellhead at 9.1 °C with the air at 4.3 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. An insulation test showed a winding fault, so the motor was replaced with an exchange unit.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 8 days longer than the repair itself because the job has to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Winding breakdown from heat and age.
