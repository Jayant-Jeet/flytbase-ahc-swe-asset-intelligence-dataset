# Maintenance Report — MNT-0089-023

**Asset:** WELL-0089 (SW-24 Well 089, T200, Southwest Block 24)
**Equipment:** wellhead
**Work started:** 21 Dec 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 31 Dec 2025 22:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 254 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman, Daniel Foster · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0089-0160 on 2025-12-18
**Report written:** 02 Jan 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the inspector saw a thin film of oil on the wellhead. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0089-0160 showed the wellhead at 12.6 °C with the air at 12.9 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The packing rings were worn and the polished rod was lightly scored.
The well stayed shut in for about 9 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part was back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Normal packing wear.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 8 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Closed the wellhead valves and bled off pressure.
2. Removed the old packing.
3. Installed new packing rings and adjusted the gland.
4. Cleaned the wellhead area.

Summary of the fix: replaced stuffing box packing.

## Testing and return to service

Checked the stuffing box with the unit running: no leak. The well was returned to production.
