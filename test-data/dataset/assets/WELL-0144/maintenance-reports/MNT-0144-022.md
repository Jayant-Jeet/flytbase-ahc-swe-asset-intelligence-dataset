# Maintenance Report — MNT-0144-022

**Asset:** WELL-0144 (NE-07 Well 144, T350, Northeast Block 7)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 27 Sep 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 04 Nov 2025 12:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 916 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Kevin Price, Daniel Foster · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0144-0150 on 2025-09-25
**Report written:** 05 Nov 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the motor breaker kept tripping and the pump jack would not run. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0144-0150 showed the wellhead at 30.3 °C with the air at 26.0 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. An insulation test showed a winding fault, so the motor was replaced with an exchange unit.
The well stayed shut in for about 36 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Winding breakdown from heat and age.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level; one crew member's monitor had not been bump-tested that day (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Disconnected and removed the motor.
2. Fitted the exchange motor on the existing base.
3. Aligned the sheaves and tensioned the belts.
4. Checked rotation direction and motor current.

Summary of the fix: replaced the electric motor with an exchange unit.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 2 hours; motor current within the nameplate rating and stroke rate normal. The well was returned to production.
