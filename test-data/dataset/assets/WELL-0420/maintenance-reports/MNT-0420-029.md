# Maintenance Report — MNT-0420-029

**Asset:** WELL-0420 (NW-12 Well 420, T500, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** esp pump
**Work started:** 21 Aug 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 29 Sep 2026 08:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 936 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0420-0198 on 2026-08-20
**Report written:** 30 Sep 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the ESP drive reported a ground fault. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0420-0198 showed the wellhead at 37.5 °C with the air at 35.0 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Insulation testing located a fault in the downhole power cable.
Coyote Ridge Well Services's workover rig was busy on other wells, so the well waited about 37 days for it; the work itself took about 2 days.

## Root cause

Cable insulation damaged by heat and gas.
This is corrective repair number 3 on this well's esp pump in the last 12 months.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- **Work stopped for H2S:** a personal monitor read 17 ppm, above the 10 ppm stop-work level. The crew moved upwind and waited about 40 minutes until readings were below 10 ppm and the HSE officer approved the restart (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Pulled the ESP string with the workover rig.
2. Located and cut out the damaged cable section.
3. Spliced in new cable and tested insulation.
4. Ran the string back in.
5. Restarted the drive.

Summary of the fix: replaced the damaged ESP power cable section.

## Testing and return to service

Insulation test passed and the ESP ran normally for 4 hours. The well was returned to production.
