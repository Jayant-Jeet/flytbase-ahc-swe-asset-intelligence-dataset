# Maintenance Report — MNT-0345-012

**Asset:** WELL-0345 (NW-12 Well 345, T350, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** flowline
**Work started:** 20 Oct 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 20 Oct 2023 19:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 11 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman, Daniel Foster
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0345-0052 on 2023-10-19
**Report written:** 20 Oct 2023

## What happened

The weekly drone inspection INS-0345-0052 at 11:00 on 2023-10-19 found crude oil on the pad: about 5.2% of the pad area, estimated 0.8 bbl. The inspector noted: "Oil sheen around cellar." The field operator shut the well in, closed the flowline valve and placed earth berms and absorbent pads. The release was contained about 7.6 hours after the inspection. The crew traced the leak to the flanged flowline connection at the wellhead cellar.

## Root cause

A failed gasket on the flange where the flowline leaves the wellhead.
This is corrective repair number 4 on this well's flowline in the last 12 months.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 6 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Cleaned free oil out of the cellar.
2. Unbolted the flange and removed the failed gasket.
3. Fitted a new gasket and new bolts, torqued to specification.
4. Excavated the stained soil around the cellar.

Summary of the fix: replaced the flange gasket and bolts and remediated soil.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure-tested the repaired section at 275 psi (1.25 × the flowline's maximum operating pressure of 220 psi) and held it for 4 hours with no pressure drop (WS-05). The well was returned to production.

## Spill clean-up and waste

- About 1.8 m³ of stained soil excavated, bagged and sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0345-012 (WS-06).
- 2 soil samples taken around the spill; the highest result after clean-up was 0.55% TPH, below the 1.0% target (SR-03).
