# Maintenance Report — MNT-0566-016

**Asset:** WELL-0566 (SE-32 Well 566, T500, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** flowline
**Work started:** 17 Jan 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 18 Jan 2025 14:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 30 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster, Megan Ward · **Contractor:** Tumbleweed Flowline Co.
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0566-0117 on 2025-01-16
**Report written:** 20 Jan 2025

## What happened

The weekly drone inspection INS-0566-0117 at 12:00 on 2025-01-16 found crude oil on the pad: about 8.1% of the pad area, estimated 4.0 bbl. The inspector noted: "Oil sheen around cellar." The field operator shut the well in, closed the flowline valve and placed earth berms and absorbent pads. The release was contained about 2.7 hours after the inspection. The crew traced the leak to the flanged flowline connection at the wellhead cellar.

## Root cause

A failed gasket on the flange where the flowline leaves the wellhead.
This is corrective repair number 3 on this well's flowline in the last 12 months.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 2 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Cleaned free oil out of the cellar.
2. Unbolted the flange and removed the failed gasket.
3. Fitted a new gasket and new bolts, torqued to specification.
4. Excavated the stained soil around the cellar.

Summary of the fix: replaced the flange gasket and bolts and remediated soil.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure-tested the repaired section at 245 psi (1.25 × the flowline's maximum operating pressure of 196 psi) and held it for 4 hours with no pressure drop (WS-05). The well was returned to production.

## Spill clean-up and waste

- About 2.8 m³ of stained soil excavated, bagged and sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0566-016 (WS-06).
- 2 soil samples taken around the spill; the highest result after clean-up was 0.35% TPH, below the 1.0% target (SR-03).
