# Maintenance Report — MNT-0240-028

**Asset:** WELL-0240 (SE-31 Well 240, T350, Southeast Block 31)
**Equipment:** wellhead
**Work started:** 05 Oct 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 22 Oct 2025 04:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 404 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Kevin Price, Brian Coleman
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0240-0152 on 2025-10-02
**Report written:** 23 Oct 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the inspector saw a thin film of oil on the wellhead. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0240-0152 showed the wellhead at 25.7 °C with the air at 21.6 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The packing rings were worn and the polished rod was lightly scored.
The well stayed shut in for about 15 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part was back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Packing wear sped up by a slightly misaligned polished rod.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Closed the wellhead valves and bled off pressure.
2. Removed the old packing.
3. Installed new packing rings and adjusted the gland.
4. Cleaned the wellhead area.

Summary of the fix: replaced stuffing box packing.

## Testing and return to service

Checked the stuffing box with the unit running: no leak. The well was returned to production.
