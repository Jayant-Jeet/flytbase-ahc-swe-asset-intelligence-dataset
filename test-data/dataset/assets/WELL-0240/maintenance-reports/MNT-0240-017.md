# Maintenance Report — MNT-0240-017

**Asset:** WELL-0240 (SE-31 Well 240, T350, Southeast Block 31)
**Equipment:** wellhead
**Work started:** 07 Jun 2024 08:00 · **Finished:** 12 Jun 2024 13:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 125 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Kevin Price
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0240-0085 on 2024-06-06
**Report written:** 13 Jun 2024

## What happened

Field staff reported that the inspector saw a thin film of oil on the wellhead. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0240-0085 showed the wellhead at 35.1 °C with the air at 30.4 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The packing rings were worn and the polished rod was lightly scored.
The well stayed shut in for about 3 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part was back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Packing wear sped up by a slightly misaligned polished rod.
This is corrective repair number 3 on this well's wellhead in the last 12 months.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 3 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Closed the wellhead valves and bled off pressure.
2. Removed the old packing.
3. Installed new packing rings and adjusted the gland.
4. Cleaned the wellhead area.

Summary of the fix: replaced stuffing box packing.

## Testing and return to service

Checked the stuffing box with the unit running: no leak. The well was returned to production.
