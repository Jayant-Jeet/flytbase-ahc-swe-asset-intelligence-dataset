## What happened

The weekly drone inspection INS-0521-0175 at 10:45 on 2027-01-14 found crude oil on the pad: about 10.6% of the pad area, estimated 5.7 bbl. The inspector noted: "Staining along flowline route." The field operator shut the well in, closed the flowline valve and placed earth berms and absorbent pads. The release was contained about 3.0 hours after the inspection. The crew traced the leak to a corrosion hole along the flowline route.

## Root cause

External corrosion where the pipe coating had been damaged.
This is corrective repair number 3 on this well's flowline in the last 12 months.
