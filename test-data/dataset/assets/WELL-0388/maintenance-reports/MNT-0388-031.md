# Maintenance Report — MNT-0388-031

**Asset:** WELL-0388 (NW-12 Well 388, T500, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** esp pump
**Work started:** 23 May 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 16 Jun 2026 05:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 573 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster, Megan Ward · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0388-0187 on 2026-05-21
**Report written:** 17 Jun 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the ESP kept tripping on start-up. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0388-0187 showed the wellhead at 44.8 °C with the air at 31.3 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Insulation testing located a fault in the downhole power cable.
Coyote Ridge Well Services's workover rig was busy on other wells, so the well waited about 22 days for it; the work itself took about 2 days.

## Root cause

Cable insulation damaged by heat and gas.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 5 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Pulled the ESP string with the workover rig.
2. Located and cut out the damaged cable section.
3. Spliced in new cable and tested insulation.
4. Ran the string back in.
5. Restarted the drive.

Summary of the fix: replaced the damaged ESP power cable section.

## Testing and return to service

Insulation test passed and the ESP ran normally for 4 hours. The well was returned to production.
