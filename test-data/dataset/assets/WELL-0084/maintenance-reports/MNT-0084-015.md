# Maintenance Report — MNT-0084-015

**Asset:** WELL-0084 (SW-24 Well 084, T200, Southwest Block 24)
**Equipment:** rod string
**Work started:** 30 Nov 2024 08:00 · **Finished:** 07 Jan 2025 21:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 925 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0084-0109 on 2024-11-28
**Report written:** 07 Jan 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the operator noticed the pump jack running unusually light and no fluid reaching the flowline. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0084-0109 showed the wellhead at 17.7 °C with the air at 13.1 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. A dynamometer card showed no load from the downhole pump, which points to a parted rod.
The company workover rig was busy on other wells, so the well waited about 37 days for it; the work itself took about 2 days.

## Root cause

Fatigue failure of a rod body, probably started by corrosion pitting.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Rigged up the company workover rig on the well.
2. Pulled the rod string and located the break.
3. Recovered the lower section with an overshot fishing tool.
4. Replaced the damaged rods.
5. Ran the rod string back in and spaced out the pump.

Summary of the fix: fished and replaced parted rod section.

## Testing and return to service

Pump test showed normal fluid delivery and the rod load returned to normal. The well was returned to production.
