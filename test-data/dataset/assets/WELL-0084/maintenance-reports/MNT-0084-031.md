# Maintenance Report — MNT-0084-031

**Asset:** WELL-0084 (SW-24 Well 084, T200, Southwest Block 24)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 22 Aug 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 08 Sep 2026 00:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 400 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Megan Ward, Kevin Price · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0084-0199 on 2026-08-20
**Report written:** 10 Sep 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator was overfilling and the dump valve was not opening. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0084-0199 showed the wellhead at 38.6 °C with the air at 36.7 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 15 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part was back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Paraffin build-up stopped the float from moving.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
