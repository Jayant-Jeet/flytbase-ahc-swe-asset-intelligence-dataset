## What happened

The weekly drone inspection INS-0399-0217 at 11:15 on 2027-01-14 found crude oil on the pad: about 0.9% of the pad area, estimated 0.9 bbl. The inspector noted: "Oil sheen around cellar." The field operator shut the well in, closed the flowline valve and placed earth berms and absorbent pads. The release was contained about 4.5 hours after the inspection. The crew traced the leak to the flanged flowline connection at the wellhead cellar.

## Root cause

A failed gasket on the flange where the flowline leaves the wellhead.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 2 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done so far

1. Cleaned free oil out of the cellar.
2. Unbolted the flange and removed the failed gasket.

Still to do: complete the remaining repair steps, test the equipment and return the well to production.

Summary of the fix: replaced the flange gasket and bolts and remediated soil.

## Testing and return to service

Not done yet. The work is expected to finish on 16 Jan 2027; testing and return to service will be recorded in the final report.

## Spill clean-up and waste

- 2 soil samples taken around the spill; the highest result after clean-up was 0.71% TPH, below the 1.0% target (SR-03).
