# Maintenance Report — MNT-0361-026

**Asset:** WELL-0361 (NW-12 Well 361, T500, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** esp pump
**Work started:** 09 Oct 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 20 Oct 2026 00:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 256 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Kevin Price, Daniel Foster
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0361-0205 on 2026-10-08
**Report written:** 22 Oct 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the ESP drive reported a ground fault. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0361-0205 showed the wellhead at 42.5 °C with the air at 27.2 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Insulation testing located a fault in the downhole power cable.
The company workover rig was busy on other wells, so the well waited about 9 days for it; the work itself took about 2 days.

## Root cause

Cable crushed against the tubing during the last installation.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- **Work stopped for H2S:** a personal monitor read 18 ppm, above the 10 ppm stop-work level. The crew moved upwind and waited about 55 minutes until readings were below 10 ppm and the HSE officer approved the restart (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Pulled the ESP string with the workover rig.
2. Located and cut out the damaged cable section.
3. Spliced in new cable and tested insulation.
4. Ran the string back in.
5. Restarted the drive.

Summary of the fix: replaced the damaged ESP power cable section.

## Testing and return to service

Insulation test passed and the ESP ran normally for 4 hours. The well was returned to production.
