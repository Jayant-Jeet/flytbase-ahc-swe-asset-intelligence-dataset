# Maintenance Report — MNT-0199-032

**Asset:** WELL-0199 (NE-08 Well 199, T200, Northeast Block 8)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 20 Nov 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 09 Dec 2026 14:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 462 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Kevin Price, Megan Ward · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0199-0210 on 2026-11-19
**Report written:** 10 Dec 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the electric motor driving the pump jack had stopped and would not restart. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0199-0210 showed the wellhead at 14.8 °C with the air at 12.6 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. An insulation test showed a winding fault, so the motor was replaced with an exchange unit.
The well stayed shut in for about 17 days longer than the repair itself because the crew was committed to a higher-priority spill response elsewhere in the field.

## Root cause

Moisture got into the motor terminal box and damaged the windings.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Disconnected and removed the motor.
2. Fitted the exchange motor on the existing base.
3. Aligned the sheaves and tensioned the belts.
4. Checked rotation direction and motor current.

Summary of the fix: replaced the electric motor with an exchange unit.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 2 hours; motor current within the nameplate rating and stroke rate normal. The well was returned to production.
