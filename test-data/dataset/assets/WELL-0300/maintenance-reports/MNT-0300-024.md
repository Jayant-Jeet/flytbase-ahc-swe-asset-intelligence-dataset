# Maintenance Report — MNT-0300-024

**Asset:** WELL-0300 (SW-24 Well 300, T350, Southwest Block 24)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 28 Dec 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 31 Dec 2025 07:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 71 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Megan Ward · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0300-0162 on 2025-12-25
**Report written:** 02 Jan 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator was overfilling and the dump valve was not opening. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0300-0162 showed the wellhead at 15.6 °C with the air at 6.0 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.

## Root cause

Controller float and switch failure.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
