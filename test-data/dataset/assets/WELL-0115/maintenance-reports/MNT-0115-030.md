# Maintenance Report — MNT-0115-030

**Asset:** WELL-0115 (NE-07 Well 115, T200, Northeast Block 7)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 14 Nov 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 24 Nov 2025 23:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 255 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0115-0160 on 2025-11-13
**Report written:** 24 Nov 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator was overfilling and the dump valve was not opening. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0115-0160 showed the wellhead at 14.3 °C with the air at 12.0 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 9 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Controller float and switch failure.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 3 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
