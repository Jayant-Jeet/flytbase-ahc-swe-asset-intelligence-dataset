# Maintenance Report — MNT-0044-009

**Asset:** WELL-0044 (SW-23 Well 044, T200, Southwest Block 23)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 06 Aug 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 30 Aug 2023 14:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 582 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Kevin Price, Brian Coleman
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0044-0042 on 2023-08-03
**Report written:** 31 Aug 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that the operator found an oil puddle under the gearbox. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0044-0042 showed the wellhead at 38.1 °C with the air at 35.6 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The input shaft seal was worn and the gearbox oil level was below the sight glass.
The well stayed shut in for about 22 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

A hardened output shaft seal after years of service.
This is corrective repair number 3 on this well's pump jack in the last 12 months.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 2 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Drained the gearbox oil into a closed container.
2. Replaced the input and output shaft seals.
3. Cleaned the base and absorbed the spilled oil.
4. Refilled with the specified gear oil to the correct level.

Summary of the fix: replaced gearbox seals and topped up oil.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 1 hour and checked both shafts for leaks: none found. The well was returned to production.

## Waste

- Used gear oil and oily pads sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0044-009 (WS-06).
