# Maintenance Report — MNT-0097-026

**Asset:** WELL-0097 (SW-24 Well 097, T350, Southwest Block 24)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 04 Jul 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 22 Jul 2025 15:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 439 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Megan Ward, Daniel Foster · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0097-0137 on 2025-07-03
**Report written:** 24 Jul 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the operator found an oil puddle under the gearbox. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0097-0137 showed the wellhead at 42.4 °C with the air at 34.5 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The input shaft seal was worn and the gearbox oil level was below the sight glass.
The well stayed shut in for about 16 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Seal wear, made worse by heat and a low oil level.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Drained the gearbox oil into a closed container.
2. Replaced the input and output shaft seals.
3. Cleaned the base and absorbed the spilled oil.
4. Refilled with the specified gear oil to the correct level.

Summary of the fix: replaced gearbox seals and topped up oil.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 1 hour and checked both shafts for leaks: none found. The well was returned to production.

## Waste

- Used gear oil and oily pads sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0097-026; the manifest was only completed the following week (WS-06).
