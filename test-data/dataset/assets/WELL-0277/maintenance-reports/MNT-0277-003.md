# Maintenance Report — MNT-0277-003

**Asset:** WELL-0277 (NE-07 Well 277, T350, Northeast Block 7)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 15 Jan 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 24 Jan 2023 14:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 222 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Megan Ward, Kevin Price · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0277-0013 on 2023-01-12
**Report written:** 25 Jan 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that the pumper heard grinding from the walking beam and saw the horsehead wobbling. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0277-0013 showed the wellhead at 10.0 °C with the air at 7.8 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. A vibration check and visual inspection found play in the saddle bearing and scoring on the bearing housing.
The well stayed shut in for about 7 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Grease contaminated with sand had worn the bearing.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Lowered the horsehead and supported the walking beam with a crane.
2. Removed the saddle bearing assembly.
3. Cleaned the housing and checked alignment.
4. Fitted a new saddle bearing and torqued the bolts to the manufacturer's values.
5. Greased all structural bearings.

Summary of the fix: replaced saddle bearing and re-greased.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 2 hours: smooth stroke, no knocking, bearing temperature normal. The well was returned to production.
