# Maintenance Report — MNT-0165-006

**Asset:** WELL-0165 (NE-08 Well 165, T200, Northeast Block 8)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 09 Sep 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 20 Sep 2023 11:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 267 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Megan Ward, Kevin Price
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0165-0046 on 2023-09-07
**Report written:** 20 Sep 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that the electric motor driving the pump jack had stopped and would not restart. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0165-0046 showed the wellhead at 31.1 °C with the air at 29.6 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. An insulation test showed a winding fault, so the motor was replaced with an exchange unit.
The well stayed shut in for about 9 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Moisture got into the motor terminal box and damaged the windings.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 4 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Disconnected and removed the motor.
2. Fitted the exchange motor on the existing base.
3. Aligned the sheaves and tensioned the belts.
4. Checked rotation direction and motor current.

Summary of the fix: replaced the electric motor with an exchange unit.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 2 hours; motor current within the nameplate rating and stroke rate normal. The well was returned to production.
