# Maintenance Report — MNT-0165-008

**Asset:** WELL-0165 (NE-08 Well 165, T200, Northeast Block 8)
**Equipment:** flowline
**Work started:** 12 Nov 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 29 Nov 2023 18:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 418 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Kevin Price
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0165-0054 on 2023-11-09
**Report written:** 30 Nov 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that the inspector saw wet soil above the buried flowline. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0165-0054 showed the wellhead at 16.6 °C with the air at 16.5 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Excavation found a corrosion pinhole on the bottom of the line.
The well stayed shut in for about 15 days longer than the repair itself because the crew was committed to a higher-priority spill response elsewhere in the field.

## Root cause

Internal corrosion from produced water collecting at a low point.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).
- Hot work permit issued for welding; gas test before welding read 0% LEL, below the 10% limit (WS-04).

## Work done

1. Excavated around the leak.
2. Cut out the corroded section.
3. Welded in a new section of line pipe.
4. Coated the new joint.
5. Backfilled after the pressure test.

Summary of the fix: cut out and replaced the corroded flowline section.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure-tested the repaired section at 265 psi (1.25 × the flowline's maximum operating pressure of 212 psi) and held it for 4 hours with no pressure drop (WS-05). The well was returned to production.

## Waste

- Oily soil from the excavation and the cut-out pipe sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0165-008 (WS-06).
