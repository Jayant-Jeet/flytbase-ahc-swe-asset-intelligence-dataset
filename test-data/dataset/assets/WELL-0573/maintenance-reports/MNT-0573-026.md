# Maintenance Report — MNT-0573-026

**Asset:** WELL-0573 (SE-32 Well 573, T350, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** flowline
**Work started:** 09 Jan 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 09 Jan 2026 23:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 15 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Kevin Price · **Contractor:** Tumbleweed Flowline Co.
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0573-0164 on 2026-01-08
**Report written:** 09 Jan 2026

## What happened

The weekly manual inspection INS-0573-0164 at 12:30 on 2026-01-08 found crude oil on the pad: about 10.4% of the pad area, estimated 5.1 bbl. The inspector noted: "Oil sheen around cellar." The field operator shut the well in, closed the flowline valve and placed earth berms and absorbent pads. The release was contained about 1.4 hours after the inspection. The crew traced the leak to the flanged flowline connection at the wellhead cellar.

## Root cause

A failed gasket on the flange where the flowline leaves the wellhead.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 2 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Cleaned free oil out of the cellar.
2. Unbolted the flange and removed the failed gasket.
3. Fitted a new gasket and new bolts, torqued to specification.
4. Excavated the stained soil around the cellar.

Summary of the fix: replaced the flange gasket and bolts and remediated soil.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure-tested the repaired section at 388 psi (1.25 × the flowline's maximum operating pressure of 310 psi) and held it for 4 hours with no pressure drop (WS-05). The well was returned to production.

## Spill clean-up and waste

- About 3.6 m³ of stained soil excavated, bagged and sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0573-026 (WS-06).
- 4 soil samples taken around the spill; the highest result after clean-up was 0.67% TPH, below the 1.0% target (SR-03).
