## What happened

Field staff reported that the operator noticed the pump jack running unusually light and no fluid reaching the flowline. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0197-0212 showed the wellhead at 9.8 °C with the air at 8.7 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. A dynamometer card showed no load from the downhole pump, which points to a parted rod.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 16 days longer than the repair itself because the workover rig is busy on other wells.

## Root cause

A coupling unscrewed after repeated rod buckling at the bottom of the stroke.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 8 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done so far

1. Rigged up the company workover rig on the well.
2. Pulled the rod string and located the break.

Still to do: complete the remaining repair steps, test the equipment and return the well to production.

Summary of the fix: fished and replaced parted rod section.
