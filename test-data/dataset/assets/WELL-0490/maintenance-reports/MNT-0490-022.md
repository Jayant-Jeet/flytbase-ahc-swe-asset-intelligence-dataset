## What happened

Field staff reported that the pump jack was running but the wellhead was cold and the rod load had dropped. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0490-0170 showed the wellhead at 12.8 °C with the air at 7.7 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. A dynamometer card showed no load from the downhole pump, which points to a parted rod.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 3 days longer than the repair itself because the workover rig is busy on other wells.

## Root cause

Fatigue failure of a rod body, probably started by corrosion pitting.
