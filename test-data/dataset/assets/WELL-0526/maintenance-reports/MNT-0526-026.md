## What happened

Field staff reported that the electric motor driving the pump jack had stopped and would not restart. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0526-0216 showed the wellhead at 6.1 °C with the air at 6.8 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. An insulation test showed a winding fault, so the motor was replaced with an exchange unit.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 16 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain is making the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Winding breakdown from heat and age.
