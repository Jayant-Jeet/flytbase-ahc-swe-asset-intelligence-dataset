# Maintenance Report — MNT-0466-016

**Asset:** WELL-0466 (SE-32 Well 466, T200, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 13 Jun 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 02 Jul 2025 15:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 463 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Kevin Price, Brian Coleman
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0466-0135 on 2025-06-12
**Report written:** 04 Jul 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0466-0135 showed the wellhead at 33.2 °C with the air at 33.9 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 17 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Paraffin build-up stopped the float from moving.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 2 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
