# Maintenance Report — MNT-0070-022

**Asset:** WELL-0070 (SW-24 Well 070, T200, Southwest Block 24)
**Equipment:** wellhead
**Work started:** 23 May 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 02 Jul 2025 12:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 964 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Megan Ward · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0070-0136 on 2025-05-22
**Report written:** 02 Jul 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that fluid was weeping from the stuffing box around the polished rod. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0070-0136 showed the wellhead at 32.7 °C with the air at 31.1 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The packing rings were worn and the polished rod was lightly scored.
The well stayed shut in for about 38 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Normal packing wear.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Closed the wellhead valves and bled off pressure.
2. Removed the old packing.
3. Installed new packing rings and adjusted the gland.
4. Cleaned the wellhead area.

Summary of the fix: replaced stuffing box packing.

## Testing and return to service

Checked the stuffing box with the unit running: no leak. The well was returned to production.
