# Maintenance Report — MNT-0070-013

**Asset:** WELL-0070 (SW-24 Well 070, T200, Southwest Block 24)
**Equipment:** flowline
**Work started:** 29 Dec 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 29 Dec 2023 22:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 14 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Kevin Price · **Contractor:** Tumbleweed Flowline Co.
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0070-0063 on 2023-12-28
**Report written:** 30 Dec 2023

## What happened

The weekly drone inspection INS-0070-0063 at 13:00 on 2023-12-28 found crude oil on the pad: about 8.1% of the pad area, estimated 2.4 bbl. The inspector noted: "Dark staining on pad." The field operator shut the well in, closed the flowline valve and placed earth berms and absorbent pads. The release was contained about 5.4 hours after the inspection. The crew traced the leak to a threaded coupling on the flowline close to the wellhead.

## Root cause

Thread fatigue from pump jack vibration.
This is corrective repair number 4 on this well's flowline in the last 12 months.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).
- Hot work permit issued for welding; gas test before welding read 0% LEL, below the 10% limit (WS-04).

## Work done

1. Excavated around the coupling.
2. Cut out the failed coupling and the pipe ends.
3. Welded in new pipe and a new fitting.
4. Coated the new joints.
5. Excavated the stained soil.
6. Backfilled after the pressure test.

Summary of the fix: replaced leaking flowline section and remediated soil.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure-tested the repaired section at 276 psi (1.25 × the flowline's maximum operating pressure of 221 psi) and held it for 4 hours with no pressure drop (WS-05). The well was returned to production.

## Spill clean-up and waste

- About 2.8 m³ of stained soil excavated, bagged and sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0070-013 (WS-06).
- 2 soil samples taken around the spill; the highest result after clean-up was 0.43% TPH, below the 1.0% target (SR-03).
