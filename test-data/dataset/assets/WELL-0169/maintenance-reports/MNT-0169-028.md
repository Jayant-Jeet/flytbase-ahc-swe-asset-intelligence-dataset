## What happened

Field staff reported that fluid was weeping from the stuffing box around the polished rod. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0169-0218 showed the wellhead at 8.0 °C with the air at 6.9 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The packing rings were worn and the polished rod was lightly scored.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 10 days longer than the repair itself because the job has to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Normal packing wear.
