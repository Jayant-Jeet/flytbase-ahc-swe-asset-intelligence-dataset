# Maintenance Report — MNT-0169-004

**Asset:** WELL-0169 (NE-08 Well 169, T200, Northeast Block 8)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 29 Apr 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 02 May 2023 16:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 80 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Megan Ward, Kevin Price
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0169-0028 on 2023-04-27
**Report written:** 02 May 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that the pump jack was running rough with a loud knock at the top of each stroke, so the operator stopped it. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0169-0028 showed the wellhead at 24.9 °C with the air at 24.3 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. A vibration check and visual inspection found play in the saddle bearing and scoring on the bearing housing.
The well stayed shut in for about 1 day longer than the repair itself because the replacement part was back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Bearing wear from long service and an overdue grease interval.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level; one crew member's monitor had not been bump-tested that day (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Lowered the horsehead and supported the walking beam with a crane.
2. Removed the saddle bearing assembly.
3. Cleaned the housing and checked alignment.
4. Fitted a new saddle bearing and torqued the bolts to the manufacturer's values.
5. Greased all structural bearings.

Summary of the fix: replaced saddle bearing and re-greased.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 2 hours: smooth stroke, no knocking, bearing temperature normal. The well was returned to production.
