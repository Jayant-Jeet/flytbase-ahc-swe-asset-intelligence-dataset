# Maintenance Report — MNT-0512-001

**Asset:** WELL-0512 (SE-32 Well 512, T350, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** rod string
**Work started:** 28 Oct 2022 08:00 · **Finished:** 14 Nov 2022 22:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 422 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Megan Ward · **Contractor:** Sandstone Rod & Pump
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0512-0002 on 2022-10-27
**Report written:** 15 Nov 2022

## What happened

Field staff reported that the pump jack was running but the wellhead was cold and the rod load had dropped. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0512-0002 showed the wellhead at 19.5 °C with the air at 16.5 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. A dynamometer card showed no load from the downhole pump, which points to a parted rod.
Sandstone Rod & Pump's workover rig was busy on other wells, so the well waited about 16 days for it; the work itself took about 2 days.

## Root cause

A coupling unscrewed after repeated rod buckling at the bottom of the stroke.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 2 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Rigged up the company workover rig on the well.
2. Pulled the rod string and located the break.
3. Recovered the lower section with an overshot fishing tool.
4. Replaced the damaged rods.
5. Ran the rod string back in and spaced out the pump.

Summary of the fix: fished and replaced parted rod section.

## Testing and return to service

Pump test showed normal fluid delivery and the rod load returned to normal. The well was returned to production.
