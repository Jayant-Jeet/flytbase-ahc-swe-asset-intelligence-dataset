## What happened

Field staff reported that the electric motor driving the pump jack had stopped and would not restart. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0316-0217 showed the wellhead at 12.9 °C with the air at 11.9 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. An insulation test showed a winding fault, so the motor was replaced with an exchange unit.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 10 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part is back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Moisture got into the motor terminal box and damaged the windings.
