## What happened

Field staff reported that the operator found an oil puddle under the gearbox. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0251-0214 showed the wellhead at 12.3 °C with the air at 11.0 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The input shaft seal was worn and the gearbox oil level was below the sight glass.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 10 days longer than the repair itself because the job has to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Seal wear, made worse by heat and a low oil level.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 4 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done so far

1. Drained the gearbox oil into a closed container.
2. Replaced the input and output shaft seals.

Still to do: complete the remaining repair steps, test the equipment and return the well to production.

Summary of the fix: replaced gearbox seals and topped up oil.
