# Maintenance Report — MNT-0410-018

**Asset:** WELL-0410 (NW-12 Well 410, T500, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** wellhead
**Work started:** 26 Apr 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 05 May 2025 20:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 228 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman, Megan Ward
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0410-0129 on 2025-04-24
**Report written:** 06 May 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that fluid was weeping around the ESP power cable penetrator on the wellhead. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0410-0129 showed the wellhead at 33.2 °C with the air at 20.0 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The penetrator seal had hardened and no longer sealed around the power cable.
The well stayed shut in for about 8 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Seal ageing from heat cycling.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Stopped the ESP drive and isolated the well.
2. Bled off wellhead pressure.
3. Replaced the penetrator seal kit.
4. Pressure tested the wellhead.
5. Restarted the drive.

Summary of the fix: replaced the cable penetrator seal.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure test passed and no weeping with the ESP running. The well was returned to production.
