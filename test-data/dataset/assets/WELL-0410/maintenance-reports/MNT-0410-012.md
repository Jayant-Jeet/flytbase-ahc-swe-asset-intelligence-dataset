# Maintenance Report — MNT-0410-012

**Asset:** WELL-0410 (NW-12 Well 410, T500, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 21 Jul 2024 08:00 · **Finished:** 23 Jul 2024 00:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 40 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Megan Ward, Daniel Foster
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0410-0090 on 2024-07-18
**Report written:** 23 Jul 2024

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator was overfilling and the dump valve was not opening. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0410-0090 showed the wellhead at 41.0 °C with the air at 37.1 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.

## Root cause

Paraffin build-up stopped the float from moving.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
