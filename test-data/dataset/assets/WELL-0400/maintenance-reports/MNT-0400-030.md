# Maintenance Report — MNT-0400-030

**Asset:** WELL-0400 (NW-12 Well 400, T500, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 19 Dec 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 05 Jan 2027 02:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 402 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0400-0215 on 2026-12-17
**Report written:** 06 Jan 2027

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator was overfilling and the dump valve was not opening. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0400-0215 showed the wellhead at 17.7 °C with the air at 8.0 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 15 days longer than the repair itself because the crew was committed to a higher-priority spill response elsewhere in the field.

## Root cause

Paraffin build-up stopped the float from moving.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level; one crew member's monitor had not been bump-tested that day (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
