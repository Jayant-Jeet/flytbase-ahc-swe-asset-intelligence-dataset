# Maintenance Report — MNT-0245-006

**Asset:** WELL-0245 (SE-31 Well 245, T500, Southeast Block 31)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 08 Oct 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 17 Oct 2023 18:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 226 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Megan Ward
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0245-0049 on 2023-10-05
**Report written:** 19 Oct 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0245-0049 showed the wellhead at 35.3 °C with the air at 25.6 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 7 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part was back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Paraffin build-up stopped the float from moving.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
