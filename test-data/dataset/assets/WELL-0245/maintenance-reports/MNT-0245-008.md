# Maintenance Report — MNT-0245-008

**Asset:** WELL-0245 (SE-31 Well 245, T500, Southeast Block 31)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 27 Oct 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 22 Nov 2023 15:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 631 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0245-0052 on 2023-10-26
**Report written:** 24 Nov 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator was overfilling and the dump valve was not opening. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0245-0052 showed the wellhead at 24.0 °C with the air at 22.7 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 24 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Paraffin build-up stopped the float from moving.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
