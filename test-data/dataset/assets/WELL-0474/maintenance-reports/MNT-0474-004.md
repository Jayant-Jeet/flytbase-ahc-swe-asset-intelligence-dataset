# Maintenance Report — MNT-0474-004

**Asset:** WELL-0474 (SE-32 Well 474, T500, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 26 Mar 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 02 May 2023 06:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 886 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Megan Ward · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0474-0023 on 2023-03-23
**Report written:** 03 May 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator was overfilling and the dump valve was not opening. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0474-0023 showed the wellhead at 22.6 °C with the air at 17.7 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 35 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Controller float and switch failure.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
