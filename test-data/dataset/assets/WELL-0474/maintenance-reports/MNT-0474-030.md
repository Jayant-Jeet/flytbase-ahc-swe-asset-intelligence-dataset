# Maintenance Report — MNT-0474-030

**Asset:** WELL-0474 (SE-32 Well 474, T500, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** wellhead
**Work started:** 18 Jul 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 21 Jul 2026 19:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 83 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Megan Ward, Brian Coleman
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0474-0191 on 2026-07-16
**Report written:** 22 Jul 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the inspector saw an oil film on the wellhead around the cable entry. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0474-0191 showed the wellhead at 49.2 °C with the air at 34.8 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The penetrator seal had hardened and no longer sealed around the power cable.
The well stayed shut in for about 1 day longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Seal damaged when the cable was last re-terminated.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Stopped the ESP drive and isolated the well.
2. Bled off wellhead pressure.
3. Replaced the penetrator seal kit.
4. Pressure tested the wellhead.
5. Restarted the drive.

Summary of the fix: replaced the cable penetrator seal.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure test passed and no weeping with the ESP running. The well was returned to production.
