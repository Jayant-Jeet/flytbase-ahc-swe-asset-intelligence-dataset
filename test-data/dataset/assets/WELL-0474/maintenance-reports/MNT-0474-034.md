# Maintenance Report — MNT-0474-034

**Asset:** WELL-0474 (SE-32 Well 474, T500, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 25 Dec 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 11 Jan 2027 20:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 420 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Kevin Price, Megan Ward
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0474-0212 on 2026-12-24
**Report written:** 13 Jan 2027

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator was overfilling and the dump valve was not opening. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0474-0212 showed the wellhead at 26.4 °C with the air at 11.9 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 16 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part was back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Paraffin build-up stopped the float from moving.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 8 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
