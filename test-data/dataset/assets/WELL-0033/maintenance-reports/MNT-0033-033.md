## What happened

Field staff reported that the pumper heard grinding from the walking beam and saw the horsehead wobbling. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0033-0221 showed the wellhead at 14.7 °C with the air at 9.4 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. A vibration check and visual inspection found play in the saddle bearing and scoring on the bearing housing.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 22 days longer than the repair itself because the crew is committed to a higher-priority spill response elsewhere in the field.

## Root cause

Grease contaminated with sand had worn the bearing.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 4 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done so far

1. Lowered the horsehead and supported the walking beam with a crane.
2. Removed the saddle bearing assembly.

Still to do: complete the remaining repair steps, test the equipment and return the well to production.

Summary of the fix: replaced saddle bearing and re-greased.
