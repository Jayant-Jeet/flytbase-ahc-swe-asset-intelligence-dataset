**Report written:** 15 Jan 2027 · **Status:** interim report, work still in progress

## What happened

Field staff reported that the pump jack was running rough with a loud knock at the top of each stroke, so the operator stopped it. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0160-0217 showed the wellhead at 15.1 °C with the air at 11.3 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. A vibration check and visual inspection found play in the saddle bearing and scoring on the bearing housing.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 9 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain is making the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Bearing wear from long service and an overdue grease interval.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 2 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done so far

1. Lowered the horsehead and supported the walking beam with a crane.
2. Removed the saddle bearing assembly.

Still to do: complete the remaining repair steps, test the equipment and return the well to production.

Summary of the fix: replaced saddle bearing and re-greased.
