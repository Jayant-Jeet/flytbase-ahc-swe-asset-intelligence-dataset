# Maintenance Report — MNT-0151-009

**Asset:** WELL-0151 (NE-08 Well 151, T350, Northeast Block 8)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 10 May 2024 08:00 · **Finished:** 21 May 2024 16:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 272 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Kevin Price, Daniel Foster
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0151-0080 on 2024-05-09
**Report written:** 21 May 2024

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0151-0080 showed the wellhead at 34.3 °C with the air at 29.7 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 9 days longer than the repair itself because the crew was committed to a higher-priority spill response elsewhere in the field.

## Root cause

Controller float and switch failure.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 2 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
