# Maintenance Report — MNT-0174-008

**Asset:** WELL-0174 (NE-08 Well 174, T350, Northeast Block 8)
**Equipment:** downhole pump
**Work started:** 29 Oct 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 30 Oct 2023 23:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 39 hours
**Crew lead:** Brian Coleman (U-304) · **Crew:** Megan Ward, Daniel Foster · **Contractor:** Sandstone Rod & Pump
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0174-0053 on 2023-10-26
**Report written:** 31 Oct 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that the wellhead was cold and the pump jack was straining with a heavy, uneven load. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0174-0053 showed the wellhead at 27.7 °C with the air at 19.8 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The downhole pump was stuck, most likely from sand or scale.

## Root cause

A worn plunger that let sand settle and lock the pump.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 5 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Rigged up the company workover rig on the well.
2. Pulled the rods and the insert pump.
3. Inspected the pump: sand in the barrel and a worn plunger.
4. Ran in a new insert pump.
5. Re-spaced the pump and hung the rods back on.

Summary of the fix: pulled and replaced downhole pump.

## Testing and return to service

Pump test confirmed fluid to surface and the wellhead warmed back to normal. The well was returned to production.
