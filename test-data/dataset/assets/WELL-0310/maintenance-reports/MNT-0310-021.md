# Maintenance Report — MNT-0310-021

**Asset:** WELL-0310 (NW-12 Well 310, T200, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 27 Dec 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 30 Dec 2025 16:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 80 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Megan Ward
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0310-0165 on 2025-12-25
**Report written:** 31 Dec 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the motor breaker kept tripping and the pump jack would not run. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0310-0165 showed the wellhead at 12.7 °C with the air at 12.8 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. An insulation test showed a winding fault, so the motor was replaced with an exchange unit.
The well stayed shut in for about 1 day longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Winding breakdown from heat and age.
This is corrective repair number 3 on this well's pump jack in the last 12 months.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 3 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Disconnected and removed the motor.
2. Fitted the exchange motor on the existing base.
3. Aligned the sheaves and tensioned the belts.
4. Checked rotation direction and motor current.

Summary of the fix: replaced the electric motor with an exchange unit.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 2 hours; motor current within the nameplate rating and stroke rate normal. The well was returned to production.
