## What happened

Field staff reported that the inspector saw wet soil above the buried flowline. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0133-0219 showed the wellhead at 12.0 °C with the air at 8.0 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Excavation found a corrosion pinhole on the bottom of the line.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 11 days longer than the repair itself because the replacement part is back-ordered from the supplier.

## Root cause

Internal corrosion from produced water collecting at a low point.
This is corrective repair number 4 on this well's flowline in the last 12 months.
