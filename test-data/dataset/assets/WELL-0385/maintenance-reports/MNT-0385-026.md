# Maintenance Report — MNT-0385-026

**Asset:** WELL-0385 (NW-12 Well 385, T200, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** wellhead
**Work started:** 31 May 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 17 Jun 2026 00:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 400 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman, Kevin Price
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0385-0185 on 2026-05-28
**Report written:** 17 Jun 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that fluid was weeping from the stuffing box around the polished rod. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0385-0185 showed the wellhead at 35.1 °C with the air at 32.7 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The packing rings were worn and the polished rod was lightly scored.
The well stayed shut in for about 15 days longer than the repair itself because the crew was committed to a higher-priority spill response elsewhere in the field.

## Root cause

Normal packing wear.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 8 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

**Follow-up:** Crew to return within 21 days to re-check the packing and wellhead valves for leaks.

## Work done

1. Closed the wellhead valves and bled off pressure.
2. Removed the old packing.
3. Installed new packing rings and adjusted the gland.
4. Cleaned the wellhead area.

Summary of the fix: replaced stuffing box packing.

## Testing and return to service

Checked the stuffing box with the unit running: no leak. The well was returned to production.
