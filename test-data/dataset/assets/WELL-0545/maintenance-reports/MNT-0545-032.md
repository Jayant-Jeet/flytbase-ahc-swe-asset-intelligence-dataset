## What happened

Field staff reported that a damp, dark patch appeared along the flowline route. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0545-0218 showed the wellhead at 13.1 °C with the air at 9.0 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Excavation found a corrosion pinhole on the bottom of the line.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 3 days longer than the repair itself because the job has to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Internal corrosion from produced water collecting at a low point.
