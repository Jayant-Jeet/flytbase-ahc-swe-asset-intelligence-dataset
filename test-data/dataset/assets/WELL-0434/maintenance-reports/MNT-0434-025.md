# Maintenance Report — MNT-0434-025

**Asset:** WELL-0434 (NW-12 Well 434, T500, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** wellhead
**Work started:** 14 Jun 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 16 Jun 2026 07:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 47 hours

**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0434-0187 on 2026-06-11
**Report written:** 18 Jun 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that the inspector saw an oil film on the wellhead around the cable entry. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0434-0187 showed the wellhead at 37.8 °C with the air at 31.2 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The penetrator seal had hardened and no longer sealed around the power cable.

## Root cause

Seal damaged when the cable was last re-terminated.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: ESP drive stopped, drive disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Stopped the ESP drive and isolated the well.
2. Bled off wellhead pressure.
3. Replaced the penetrator seal kit.
4. Pressure tested the wellhead.
5. Restarted the drive.

Summary of the fix: replaced the cable penetrator seal.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure test passed and no weeping with the ESP running. The well was returned to production.
