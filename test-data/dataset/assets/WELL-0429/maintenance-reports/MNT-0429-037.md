## What happened

Field staff reported that the wellhead was cold and the pump jack was straining with a heavy, uneven load. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0429-0219 showed the wellhead at 18.0 °C with the air at 11.8 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The downhole pump was stuck, most likely from sand or scale.
The well is expected to stay shut in for about 3 days longer than the repair itself because the workover rig is busy on other wells.

## Root cause

Free gas entering the pump stopped it from filling, and the dry plunger seized.
