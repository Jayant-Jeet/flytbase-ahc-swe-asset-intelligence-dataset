# Maintenance Report — MNT-0326-021

**Asset:** WELL-0326 (NW-12 Well 326, T350, Northwest Block 12)
**Equipment:** flowline
**Work started:** 15 May 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 27 May 2026 20:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 300 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Kevin Price, Daniel Foster · **Contractor:** Tumbleweed Flowline Co.
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0326-0186 on 2026-05-14
**Report written:** 28 May 2026

## What happened

Field staff reported that a damp, dark patch appeared along the flowline route. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0326-0186 showed the wellhead at 28.3 °C with the air at 26.7 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. Excavation found a corrosion pinhole on the bottom of the line.
The well stayed shut in for about 10 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Internal corrosion from produced water collecting at a low point.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).
- Hot work permit issued for welding; gas test before welding read 3% LEL, below the 10% limit (WS-04).

**Follow-up:** Crew to return within 7 days to check the soil around the repair after the next rain.

## Work done

1. Excavated around the leak.
2. Cut out the corroded section.
3. Welded in a new section of line pipe.
4. Coated the new joint.
5. Backfilled after the pressure test.

Summary of the fix: cut out and replaced the corroded flowline section.

## Testing and return to service

Pressure-tested the repaired section at 299 psi (1.25 × the flowline's maximum operating pressure of 239 psi) and held it for 4 hours with no pressure drop (WS-05). The well was returned to production.

## Waste

- Oily soil from the excavation and the cut-out pipe sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0326-021 (WS-06).
