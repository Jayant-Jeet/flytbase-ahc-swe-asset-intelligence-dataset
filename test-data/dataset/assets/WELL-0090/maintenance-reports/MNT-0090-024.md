# Maintenance Report — MNT-0090-024

**Asset:** WELL-0090 (SW-24 Well 090, T350, Southwest Block 24)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 13 Sep 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 24 Sep 2025 17:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 273 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Megan Ward · **Contractor:** Coyote Ridge Well Services
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0090-0149 on 2025-09-11
**Report written:** 26 Sep 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0090-0149 showed the wellhead at 27.7 °C with the air at 28.4 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 9 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Controller float and switch failure.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 3 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
