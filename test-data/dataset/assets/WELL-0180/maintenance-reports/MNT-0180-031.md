# Maintenance Report — MNT-0180-031

**Asset:** WELL-0180 (NE-08 Well 180, T200, Northeast Block 8)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 29 Nov 2026 08:00 · **Finished:** 06 Jan 2027 16:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 920 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Daniel Foster
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0180-0213 on 2026-11-26
**Report written:** 07 Jan 2027

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator high-level alarm stayed on. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0180-0213 showed the wellhead at 18.2 °C with the air at 15.5 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 36 days longer than the repair itself because the job had to wait for a weekday contractor slot.

## Root cause

Paraffin build-up stopped the float from moving.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 3 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
