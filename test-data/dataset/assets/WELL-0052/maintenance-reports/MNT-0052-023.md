# Maintenance Report — MNT-0052-023

**Asset:** WELL-0052 (SW-24 Well 052, T200, Southwest Block 24)
**Equipment:** separator
**Work started:** 14 Sep 2025 08:00 · **Finished:** 23 Sep 2025 00:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 208 hours
**Crew lead:** Daniel Foster (U-302) · **Crew:** Brian Coleman
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0052-0150 on 2025-09-11
**Report written:** 23 Sep 2025

## What happened

Field staff reported that the separator was overfilling and the dump valve was not opening. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0052-0150 showed the wellhead at 27.3 °C with the air at 26.6 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The level controller had failed.
The well stayed shut in for about 7 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Controller float and switch failure.
This is corrective repair number 3 on this well's separator in the last 12 months.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Isolated and depressurised the separator.
2. Replaced the level controller.
3. Calibrated the controller and tested the dump valve.

Summary of the fix: replaced level controller.

## Testing and return to service

Cycled the separator three times; level control worked correctly. The well was returned to production.
