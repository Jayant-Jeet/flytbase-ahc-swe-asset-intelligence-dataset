# Maintenance Report — MNT-0510-004

**Asset:** WELL-0510 (SE-32 Well 510, T350, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** rod string
**Work started:** 18 Jun 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 20 Jun 2023 21:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 61 hours
**Crew lead:** Megan Ward (U-305) · **Crew:** Kevin Price, Daniel Foster
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0510-0035 on 2023-06-15
**Report written:** 26 Jun 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that the pump jack was running but the wellhead was cold and the rod load had dropped. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0510-0035 showed the wellhead at 38.3 °C with the air at 36.1 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. A dynamometer card showed no load from the downhole pump, which points to a parted rod.

## Root cause

Fatigue failure of a rod body, probably started by corrosion pitting.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 1 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Rigged up the company workover rig on the well.
2. Pulled the rod string and located the break.
3. Recovered the lower section with an overshot fishing tool.
4. Replaced the damaged rods.
5. Ran the rod string back in and spaced out the pump.

Summary of the fix: fished and replaced parted rod section.

## Testing and return to service

Pump test showed normal fluid delivery and the rod load returned to normal. The well was returned to production.
