# Maintenance Report — MNT-0537-008

**Asset:** WELL-0537 (SE-32 Well 537, T350, Southeast Block 32)
**Equipment:** pump jack
**Work started:** 22 Dec 2023 08:00 · **Finished:** 26 Dec 2023 14:00 · **Well stopped for this job:** 102 hours
**Crew lead:** Kevin Price (U-303) · **Crew:** Megan Ward, Daniel Foster
**Triggered by:** inspection INS-0537-0061 on 2023-12-21
**Report written:** 27 Dec 2023

## What happened

Field staff reported that oil was dripping from the gearbox onto the pump jack base. The thermal reading at inspection INS-0537-0061 showed the wellhead at 18.1 °C with the air at 7.7 °C, so the well was not producing. The operator shut the well in for the repair. The input shaft seal was worn and the gearbox oil level was below the sight glass.
The well stayed shut in for about 2 days longer than the repair itself because heavy rain made the lease road impassable for the service truck.

## Root cause

Seal wear, made worse by heat and a low oil level.
This is corrective repair number 3 on this well's pump jack in the last 12 months.

## Safety and permits

- Pre-job safety meeting held with the crew before work started, but it was not written down (WS-01).
- Energy isolated: prime mover stopped at the bottom of the stroke, brake set and chained, motor disconnect locked and tagged, wellhead wing valve closed and tagged, pressure bled off (WS-02).
- Personal H2S monitors worn; highest reading 0 ppm, below the 10 ppm stop-work level (WS-03).

## Work done

1. Drained the gearbox oil into a closed container.
2. Replaced the input and output shaft seals.
3. Cleaned the base and absorbed the spilled oil.
4. Refilled with the specified gear oil to the correct level.

Summary of the fix: replaced gearbox seals and topped up oil.

## Testing and return to service

Ran the unit for 1 hour and checked both shafts for leaks: none found. The well was returned to production.

## Waste

- Used gear oil and oily pads sent to an approved disposal site under waste manifest WM-0537-008 (WS-06).
