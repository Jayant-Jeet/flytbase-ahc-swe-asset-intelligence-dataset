# T350 Deep Rod-Lift Oil Well — Operating & Maintenance Manual

> Fictional manual written for the FlytBase Asset Copilot hackathon. Not for real-world use.

Document ID: OM-T350 · Revision 2 · Applies to all wells with `well_type = "T350"`

## 1. Scope

The T350 is a deeper, higher-rate version of the T200 rod-lift well. It uses a
WH-5000 wellhead, a PJ-456 pump jack and the same rod, pump and
flowline families as the T200. Procedures not listed here follow the T200 manual.

## 2. Differences from T200

| Item | T200 | T350 |
|---|---|---|
| Wellhead rating | 3,000 psi | 5,000 psi |
| Pump jack | PJ-228 | PJ-456 |
| Normal stroke rate | 6–9 spm | 5–8 spm |
| Max polished-rod load | 22,800 lb | 45,600 lb |

## 3. Production temperature rule

Because T350 fluid comes from a deeper, hotter zone, a T350 well is considered
**producing (active)** only when its wellhead temperature is at least **20 °C above
ambient** air temperature at the time of the reading.

## 4. Temperature alarm

The wellhead must stay **at or below 95 °C**. Above 95 °C, shut in and inspect within 24 hours.

## 5. Spill-related shut-in

Same as T200: a spill covering more than 5 % of the pad area requires immediate shut-in.

## 6. Maintenance escalation

Same as T200 section 10 (RCA after three corrective events on the same equipment
kind within 12 months; report downtime over 14 consecutive days).
