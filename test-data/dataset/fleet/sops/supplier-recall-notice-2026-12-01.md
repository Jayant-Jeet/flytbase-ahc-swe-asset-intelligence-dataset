# Supplier Recall Notice — Downhole Rod Pumps

> Fictional notice for the FlytBase Asset Copilot hackathon.

**From:** Highline Pump Co., Quality Department
**To:** All operators running Highline downhole rod pumps
**Date:** 1 Dec 2026
**Reference:** HPC-RN-2026-07

## What is recalled

Highline RP-175 downhole rod pumps from these manufacturing lots:

- LOT-2409-A
- LOT-2409-C
- LOT-2410-B

Pumps from all other lots, including LOT-2409-B, LOT-2410-A and LOT-2410-C, are not affected.
Highline Pump Co. does not supply ESP pumps, so ESP wells are not affected by this notice.

## The problem

Testing found that plungers from these lots can crack after several months in service. A cracked
plunger lets the pump lose fluid and can break apart downhole, which makes the pump much harder
to pull.

## What you must do

Pumps from these lots must be pulled and replaced within 30 days of this notice (by 31 Dec 2026).

Until the replacement, keep running the pump as normal. Do not shut the well in only because of
this notice; the risk of cracking is low over a few weeks.

## How to find affected pumps

The lot number is stamped on the pump barrel. Your crews record it when the pump is installed
(in your maintenance reports, on the line "Parts installed"). Only the pump that is in the well
today matters: a recalled pump that has already been pulled and replaced needs no action.

Return pulled pumps to Highline Pump Co. for a full credit.
