> Fictional memo for the FlytBase Asset Copilot hackathon.

**Title:** Temporary flowline bypass while check valves are on back-order
**Date:** 19 Oct 2026
**Requested by:** Robert Hayes (U-301), maintenance lead
**Approved by:** Emily Carter (U-101), production supervisor; Laura Simmons (U-401), HSE

## Change

A temporary flowline bypass is authorized on these wells: WELL-0013, WELL-0062, WELL-0118, WELL-0248, WELL-0476.

The bypass routes production around a leaking check valve so the flowline can stay in service
until the replacement valve arrives. It must be removed as soon as the valve is replaced.

## Conditions

- Effective from 20 Oct 2026.
- The bypass is checked on every daily round and recorded as an operator note on the well when it
  is put in and when it is taken out.
- Only the wells named above are covered. Any other well needs its own MOC.

This approval expires on 30 Nov 2026.
