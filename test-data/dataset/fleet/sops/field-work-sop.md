# Field Work Safety and Maintenance SOP

> Fictional SOP for the FlytBase Asset Copilot hackathon. Simplified and modelled on common
> US onshore practice and the public scope of industry documents named below. It is not a
> copy of any standard and is not real safety, legal or regulatory guidance.

Document ID: SOP-WS · Revision 2 · Owner: Field Maintenance (TEAM-MX) with HSE (TEAM-HSE)

## 1. Purpose

Defines how maintenance crews prepare for, carry out, test and close out work on a well:
the pump jack, rod string, downhole pump, wellhead, flowline and separator. Every
maintenance report must include a compliance check against the protocols below.

## 2. Background (industry references)

| Topic | What industry practice says | How this SOP uses it |
|---|---|---|
| Crew safety on well servicing | API RP 54 (*Occupational Safety and Health for Oil and Gas Well Drilling and Servicing Operations*) covers well servicing rigs and special services. Pre-job safety meetings (job safety analysis briefings) are standard practice in OSHA's oil and gas safety guidance. | WS-01 |
| Energy isolation | The federal lockout/tagout standard (OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147) lists oil and gas well drilling and servicing as outside its scope; the US Chemical Safety Board has recommended closing that gap. API RP 11G (*Installation, Maintenance and Lubrication of Pumping Units*) covers safe installation and servicing of beam pumping units, and the manufacturer's instructions take precedence. | WS-02 is our company rule |
| Hydrogen sulfide | API RP 49 covers drilling and well servicing where hydrogen sulfide (H2S) may be present; 10 ppm is the commonly used action level. | WS-03 |
| Hot work | API RP 2009 covers safe welding, cutting and hot work in the petroleum industry, including permits and gas testing. | WS-04 (the 10% LEL limit is our company rule) |
| Pressure testing | US rules for hazardous liquid pipelines (49 CFR 195.304) test at 1.25 × the maximum operating pressure (MOP) for at least 4 hours. ASME B31.4 bases its test on the internal design pressure. Flowlines on a lease are not always covered by these rules. | WS-05 is our company rule, modelled on 49 CFR 195.304 |
| Flowline maintenance | EPA SPCC rules for onshore production (40 CFR 112.9(d)(4)) require a flowline maintenance program: compatible materials, scheduled inspection or testing, corrective action, and prompt cleanup of discharges. | WS-05, WS-06 and RC-05 |

## 3. Protocols

| Protocol ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **WS-01** | Hold and record a **pre-job safety meeting** (job safety analysis) with the whole crew before any work starts. |
| **WS-02** | **Isolate energy** before touching moving or pressurised equipment. Stop the prime mover at the bottom of the stroke, set and chain the brake, lock and tag the motor disconnect, close and tag the wellhead wing valve, and bleed trapped pressure. Remove locks only after the crew lead confirms everyone is clear. |
| **WS-03** | Every crew member wears a **personal H2S monitor**, bump-tested that day. If any monitor reads **10 ppm or more**, stop work, move upwind, and do not return until readings are below 10 ppm and the HSE officer approves. |
| **WS-04** | Welding, cutting or grinding needs a **hot work permit** and a **gas test** first. No hot work while flammable gas reads **10% of the lower explosive limit (LEL) or more**. |
| **WS-05** | After any flowline repair, **pressure-test** the repaired section at **at least 1.25 × the flowline's maximum operating pressure (MOP)** and **hold for 4 hours** with no unexplained pressure drop before returning the well to production. |
| **WS-06** | Contaminated soil, absorbents and replaced parts with oil on them are **waste**. Bag or contain them on site, record the quantity, and ship them with a **waste manifest** to an approved disposal site. |
| **WS-07** | Write the **maintenance report** within 3 days of finishing the work. Include what happened, root cause, work done, parts, safety steps, tests, waste and a compliance check against this SOP and the Spill Response SOP. |

## 4. Typical work by equipment

| Equipment | Usual problems | Usual work |
|---|---|---|
| Pump jack | Bearing wear, gearbox oil leaks, prime mover (electric motor) trips or failure, belt wear | Replace bearings or seals, change gearbox oil, rewind or replace motor, re-tension belts, check counterbalance |
| Rod string | Parted (broken) sucker rod | Workover rig pulls rods, fishing tool recovers the broken part, damaged rods replaced |
| Downhole pump | Stuck or worn pump | Workover rig pulls rods and pump, pump replaced, well tested |
| Wellhead | Stuffing box packing leak, valve wear | Replace packing, service or replace valves, pressure test |
| Flowline | Corrosion pinhole leaks, coupling failures | Clamp or replace the section, pressure test (WS-05), clean up any release (SR-03) |
| Separator | Level controller faults | Replace or recalibrate controller, test dump valve |

## 5. Close-out

The crew lead confirms the well is producing again (or records why not), removes locks and
tags, and files the report (WS-07). HSE reviews every report with a "not met" item.
