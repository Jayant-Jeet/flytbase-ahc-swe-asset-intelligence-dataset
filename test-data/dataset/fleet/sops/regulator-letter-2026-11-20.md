# Letter from the State Oil & Gas Commission

> Fictional letter for the FlytBase Asset Copilot hackathon.

**From:** State Oil & Gas Commission, District Office
**To:** Northeast Production (TEAM-NE), attention Michael Turner, production supervisor
**Date:** 20 Nov 2026
**Subject:** Soil sampling after reportable spills in Northeast Block 7 and Northeast Block 8

Our review of recent spill reports from your Northeast Block 7 and Northeast Block 8 leases found
clean-up files without laboratory soil results.

For every reportable spill in Northeast Block 7 or Northeast Block 8 since 1 Jun 2026, soil samples must be taken during clean-up and results submitted by 15 Jan 2027.

"Reportable" has the meaning in your Spill Response SOP (5 bbl or more). Spills that you later
confirmed were not crude oil do not need samples. Failure to submit results may lead to a notice of
violation.

---

**Internal note from HSE (Laura Simmons, U-401), 22 Nov 2026:** crews must record lab samples in the
"Spill clean-up and waste" section of the maintenance report. We treat a spill as compliant only if
its clean-up report contains the sentence "Soil samples sent to the lab." Field soil readings taken
on site do not count.
